The Era is PocketBook's 7-inch reader, and its stand-out feature is a real
built-in speaker on top of Bluetooth — few e-readers have one. Add side
page-turn buttons, IPX8 waterproofing, open format support and on-device
Adobe-DRM library books, and it is one of the most capable mid-size readers on
paper.

- **Kobo Libra Colour.** The same 7-inch size with buttons and waterproofing,
  plus a colour screen and stylus support, but it is a dedicated Kobo — no
  built-in speaker, audio limited to Kobo store audiobooks over Bluetooth, and a
  closed format list. Choose the Libra Colour for colour, note-taking and Kobo's
  polished store; choose the Era for the speaker, open audio and format freedom.
- **PocketBook Verse Pro.** The same software and audio features in a smaller
  6-inch body, but with no speaker — Bluetooth only — and a smaller battery.
  Choose the Verse Pro for pocketability and a lower price; choose the Era for
  the larger page, the speaker and the bigger battery.
- **Kobo Sage.** An 8-inch Kobo with buttons, waterproofing and stylus support.
  It is larger and pricier, better for note-taking and PDFs, but again with no
  speaker and Kobo-only audio. Choose the Sage for the big screen and stylus;
  choose the Era for audio and a lighter device.
