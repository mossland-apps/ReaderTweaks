KOReader is a separate reading application that runs on the Sage without removing
anything. When you install it, the Kobo home screen gains a "KOReader" icon;
tapping it starts KOReader, and closing it returns you to the normal Kobo
interface (called **Nickel**).

## Why put it on the Sage

- **Typography control** — margins, line spacing, hyphenation and font-weight
  settings that Nickel keeps hidden, which help on the large 8-inch page.
- **PDF handling** — KOReader's reflow and crop tools are stronger than Kobo's
  for documents, and the Sage's screen is a good size for them.
- **Configurable buttons and gestures** — every press and swipe can be assigned
  an action, which Kobo's software does not allow.
- **One setup across devices**, with a synced reading position.

## What you give up inside KOReader

The Kobo store, OverDrive borrowing, Bluetooth audiobooks, and — importantly for
the Sage — the stylus and notebooks do not work in KOReader. KOReader has no
handwriting support. If you switch to KOReader for reading, you switch back to
Nickel to take notes.
