The Paper Pro Move is a writing tablet, so these guides are about documents and
notes rather than an ebook library. This section covers setting up a new one,
the several ways to get PDFs and EPUBs onto it, and how the notebooks and
handwriting conversion work at pocket size.
[Calibre](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pro-move/calibre/) and
[KOReader](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pro-move/koreader/) have their
own sections;
[Customization](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pro-move/customization/)
covers templates and writing settings.
