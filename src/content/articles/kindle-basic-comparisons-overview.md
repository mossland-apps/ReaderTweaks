The entry Kindle's case is simple: the cheapest way into Amazon's ecosystem, on a
sharp 6-inch screen. Everything below adds something for more money.

- **Kindle Paperwhite.** The obvious upsell within Kindle: a larger 7-inch
  screen, a warm light, water resistance and more storage. If any of those
  matter, the Paperwhite is worth the difference. If you just want to read text
  and price is the priority, the entry Kindle does that fine.
- **Kobo Clara BW.** The entry Kindle's closest rival on price and size — also a
  6-inch reader, and it has a warm light, which the basic Kindle does not. The
  real difference is the ecosystem: the Clara BW takes sideloaded EPUB and
  KOReader easily, while the Kindle means conversion and jailbreaking. Choose the
  Kindle if you buy from Amazon; choose the Clara BW to keep your library open.
- **Kobo Libra Colour.** A step further up: 7-inch, colour, physical page-turn
  buttons, waterproof, stylus, open ecosystem. Compare it to the Paperwhite
  rather than the entry Kindle unless budget is no object.
