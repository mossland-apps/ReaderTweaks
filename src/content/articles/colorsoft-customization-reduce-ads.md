steps:
  - instruction: Check whether your Kindle has ads at all
    detail: >-
      Amazon lists the 2026 Colorsoft as having no lock-screen ads. Some earlier
      Colorsoft units were sold "with Special Offers". On the Kindle, open
      Settings and look for a "Special Offers" entry. If there is none, you have
      the ad-free version and there is nothing to do.
  - instruction: Understand what the ads are
    detail: >-
      A "with Special Offers" Kindle shows a full-screen advertisement while it
      sleeps and a small banner at the bottom of the home screen. Ads never
      appear while you are reading. Amazon sold the device at a lower price in
      exchange.
  - instruction: Remove the ads by paying the one-time fee
    detail: >-
      On a computer, go to "Manage Your Content and Devices" in your Amazon
      account and open the Devices tab. Select your Colorsoft. Find the "Special
      Offers" line and choose the option to unsubscribe. Amazon charges a
      one-time fee, and the change is permanent for that device.
  - instruction: Sync the Kindle so the change takes effect
    detail: >-
      Turn Wi-Fi on and choose "Sync" from the quick settings or Settings menu,
      or restart the device. The ads disappear within a minute or two of
      syncing.
  - instruction: If you are not going to pay — what you can still do
    detail: >-
      You cannot replace the ads with your own images or turn them off in
      settings. You can make them less intrusive: keep the Kindle in a case that
      sleeps and wakes it, so you see the ad for less time, and turn off
      "Special Offers notifications" under Settings, then Notifications.
---

Whether a Colorsoft has lock-screen ads depends on which one you bought, so
check Settings first — many owners have nothing to remove. If yours does show
"Special Offers", the only real fix is the one-time payment through Amazon's
website, which converts the device to the ad-free version for good.
