The Palma 2 is a pocket device carried like a phone, so protection and grip
matter more than on a desk-bound reader. A case that sleeps and wakes it on open
and close saves battery and guards the screen. A microSD card keeps a large
sideloaded library separate from app data and safe through a factory reset. A
lanyard or grip makes one-handed reading on the go less precarious. Bluetooth
earbuds are the way to hear audiobooks or text-to-speech away from the built-in
speaker. Picks are grouped by type below.
