steps:
  - instruction: Charge the Paper Pure before setup
    detail: >-
      A new device often ships part-charged, and setup includes a software
      update. Plug it in with the supplied USB-C cable and give it time on the
      charger. The battery is large — reMarkable rates it for weeks of use — so a
      full charge takes a while.
  - instruction: Turn it on, connect to Wi-Fi, and create a reMarkable account
    detail: >-
      Press the power button on the top edge. Choose your language, connect to
      Wi-Fi, and create or sign in to a reMarkable account. Unlike a Kindle or
      Kobo, an account is effectively required — it is how the device syncs and
      how the apps connect.
  - instruction: Understand the reMarkable Connect subscription
    detail: >-
      The device works without a subscription for on-device writing and reading.
      A paid "reMarkable Connect" plan adds unlimited cloud sync history, the
      mobile and desktop apps' full features, handwriting-to-text in more places,
      and integrations with Google Drive, Dropbox and OneNote. New devices
      usually include a free trial. Decide later; nothing is lost by skipping it
      at first.
  - instruction: Let the software update install
    detail: >-
      The Paper Pure checks for and installs the latest firmware and restarts.
      Updates regularly add note-taking features, so keep automatic updates on
      unless you are running community modifications.
  - instruction: Pair and check the Marker
    detail: >-
      The Marker is battery-free and pairs the first time you touch it to the
      screen. It attaches magnetically to the right edge, which also charges the
      current-generation Marker wirelessly. The Paper Pure does not work with the
      reMarkable 2's older pens. If you have the Marker Plus, the top end is an
      eraser.
  - instruction: There is no front light — set expectations
    detail: >-
      Unlike the Paper Pro, the Paper Pure has no front light at all. It is a
      pure reflective screen, like a paper notebook or a basic Kindle from years
      ago. Plan to use it in ambient light, and consider a clip-on light if you
      write in bed.
  - instruction: Install the desktop and mobile apps
    detail: >-
      On your computer and phone, install the reMarkable app and sign in with the
      same account. The desktop app is the main way to move PDFs onto the device;
      the mobile app is handy for sending a document from your phone and for
      reading your notes elsewhere.
  - instruction: Set handedness
    detail: >-
      In Settings, set your writing hand so the floating toolbar and palm
      rejection sit on the correct side. Left-handed users should do this first —
      the defaults assume right.
---

Setting up the Paper Pure is straightforward, with two things that differ from a
normal e-reader: an account is essentially required, and there is an optional
paid subscription (Connect) for the fuller cloud and integration features. The
one hardware fact to absorb is that there is no front light — the Paper Pure is
a deliberately plain, reflective writing surface.
