PocketBook's software is more open than Kobo's or Amazon's: you can copy almost
any book file straight onto the device and it will open. On the InkPad 4 the
7.8-inch screen changes what is comfortable to read — full-size PDFs and comics
work here in a way they do not on a 6-inch reader. This section covers setting up
a new one, loading books, getting the most from documents, and its
speaker-and-Bluetooth audio. The
[Calibre](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-inkpad-4/calibre/) and
[KOReader](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-inkpad-4/koreader/) sections cover
those; [Customization](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-inkpad-4/customization/)
covers fonts, remapping the buttons and the sleep screen.
