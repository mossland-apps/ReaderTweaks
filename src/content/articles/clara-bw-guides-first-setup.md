  - instruction: Charge the Clara BW before the first setup
    detail: >-
      A new device often ships with a low battery, and setup includes a software
      update that should not be interrupted. Plug it in with the supplied USB-C

  - instruction: Turn it on and choose your language
    detail: >-
      Press the power button on the bottom edge for a couple of seconds. The
      first screen is a language list; this sets the menu language, not the
      language of your books.
  - instruction: Connect to Wi-Fi
    detail: >-
      Pick your network and enter its password. The Clara BW needs this once to
      activate and download its current firmware. After setup you can leave
      Wi-Fi off to save battery and turn it on only when you want to sync or
      shop.
  - instruction: Let the firmware update finish
    detail: >-
      The device downloads and installs the latest Kobo software, shows a
      progress bar, and restarts once. This can take five to ten minutes. Leave
      it plugged in and do not press anything until it returns to the setup
      screens.
  - instruction: Decide whether to sign in with a Kobo account
    detail: >-
      A Kobo account is only needed to buy or borrow books through the Kobo store
      and the built-in OverDrive library feature. It is not required to read your
      own EPUB files. You can sign in with an existing Kobo, Google or Apple
      account, or look for the option to skip and set the device up without one.
    tip: >-
      If you mainly plan to sideload books with Calibre or use KOReader, skipping
      the account keeps setup simple. You can always sign in later from Settings.
  - instruction: Set the front light and warm light to taste
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top of the screen to open the quick settings. The
      brightness slider controls the white front light; the second slider adds a
      warm amber tone, which most people raise in the evening. There is also a
      schedule that shifts it automatically after sunset.
  - instruction: Add your books
    detail: >-
      Connect the Clara BW to a computer with the USB-C cable and copy EPUB
      files into any folder on the drive, or use Calibre for a tidier workflow.
      On the home screen, open "My Books" to see them.

Setting up a Clara BW is short, and the only decision that trips people up is
the account step. Nothing about the account is mandatory for reading books you
already own — it exists for the Kobo store, Kobo's reading-stats sync, and
borrowing from a public library through OverDrive. If none of those matter to
you, set the device up without an account and move straight to adding your own
books.
