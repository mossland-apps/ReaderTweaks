The Paper Pure is reMarkable's plain 10.3-inch writing tablet — no front light,
no colour, no distractions — on newer internals than the reMarkable 2 and with a
repairable, replaceable-battery design. It is a poor ebook reader, and some
cloud features need a subscription.

- **reMarkable 2.** The device the Paper Pure replaces. The reading and writing
  experience is very close; the Paper Pure adds a faster processor, more storage,
  a bigger replaceable battery, and repairability, while the reMarkable 2 has a
  far more mature community-modification scene (KOReader through Toltec is rock
  solid on it). Choose the Paper Pure new; choose a used or refurbished
  reMarkable 2 to save money or if you specifically want the settled KOReader
  setup.
- **Kindle Scribe.** Also a 10-inch writing tablet, but a full Kindle too, so it
  reads Amazon books well and has a front light. Its notebooks live inside
  Amazon's system with email-only export. Choose the Scribe if you want one
  device for Kindle books and notes; choose the Paper Pure for a more paper-like
  writing feel, deeper note organisation and open export — accepting the weak
  reader and no light.
- **reMarkable Paper Pro.** The same software with an 11.8-inch colour screen and
  a front light, for more money and weight. Choose the Paper Pro if you want
  colour or the bigger page and a light; choose the Paper Pure for a lighter,
  cheaper, plainer writing slate.
