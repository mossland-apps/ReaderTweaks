The Note Air 4 C is a full Android device with a colour e-ink screen and a pen,
so the work is choosing and tuning apps and learning the note-taking tools —
not fighting a locked ecosystem. This section covers setting up a new one,
installing the reading apps you want, the display settings that make Android
apps usable on e-ink, and how the stylus and markup work.
[KOReader](/devices/boox/boox-note-air-4-c/koreader/) and
[Calibre](/devices/boox/boox-note-air-4-c/calibre/) have their own sections;
[Customization](/devices/boox/boox-note-air-4-c/customization/) covers fonts,
gestures, the per-app optimisation dialog and note templates.
