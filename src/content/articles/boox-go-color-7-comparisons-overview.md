The Go Color 7's case is flexibility: full Android, the Play Store, any reading
app, a microSD slot, and no store lock-in. The trade-off is that it takes setup
to look and behave like a good e-reader, and its standby battery life is shorter
than a dedicated reader's.

- **Kobo Libra Colour.** The closest comparison — also 7-inch, colour, with
  page-turn buttons — but a dedicated reader, not Android. It works well out of
  the box, is waterproof, and has far better standby. It cannot run the Kindle
  app or arbitrary apps. Choose the Libra Colour if you want it to just work and
  mostly live in the Kobo or KOReader world; choose the Go Color 7 if you need
  the Kindle app, Libby, or apps in general on one device.
- **Kobo Clara BW.** Smaller, cheaper, black-and-white, no buttons, no Android.
  A simple, long-lasting plain reader. Choose it if you want the opposite of the
  Go Color 7 — nothing to configure.
- **Kindle Paperwhite.** A locked 7-inch Amazon reader, waterproof, with the best
  standby of the four. Choose it only if you buy from the Kindle store and want
  simplicity. The Go Color 7 can run the Kindle app and everything else besides.
