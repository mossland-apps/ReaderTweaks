The Scribe is a closed device, so the guides are about what it does rather than
how to change it. This section covers setting up a new one, loading your own
books and PDFs, and the note-taking that the pen exists for.
[Calibre](/devices/kindle/scribe/calibre/) covers library management on a
computer; [Customization](/devices/kindle/scribe/customization/) covers fonts
and notebook templates; [KOReader](/devices/kindle/scribe/koreader/) explains
why it is not a practical option on this device.
