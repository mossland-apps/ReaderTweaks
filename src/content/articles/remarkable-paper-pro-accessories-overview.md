The Paper Pro is large and comes with its Marker, so the accessories that matter
are about protecting it and keeping it writing. A folio is the first purchase.
Replacement Marker tips are a consumable you will need regularly. The Type Folio
adds a physical keyboard for people who draft text as well as handwrite. Picks
are grouped by type below, with a note on which are cut for this device and which
fit any reader.
