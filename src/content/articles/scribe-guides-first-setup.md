steps:
  - instruction: Charge the Scribe before setup
    detail: >-
      A new Scribe usually ships part-charged, and setup includes a software
      update. Plug it in with the supplied USB-C cable and give it time on the
      charger before you start. The battery is large, so a full charge takes a
      while.
  - instruction: Turn it on and connect to Wi-Fi
    detail: >-
      Hold the power button on the top edge. Choose your language, then your
      Wi-Fi network and enter its password. The Scribe needs Wi-Fi once to
      register and download its current firmware.
  - instruction: Sign in to your Amazon account
    detail: >-
      Enter the email and password for the Amazon account the Scribe should be
      tied to. This is "registering" the device. A registered Scribe can
      download your purchases, use Send to Kindle, and sync notebooks to the
      Kindle apps. An unregistered one only reads books you copy over by cable.
    tip: >-
      If the Scribe was a gift and is registered to someone else's account, go to
      Settings, then Your Account, choose Deregister, and register it to your
      own.
  - instruction: Let the software update install
    detail: >-
      The Scribe checks for and installs the latest firmware and restarts once.
      Software updates for the Scribe regularly add note-taking features, so it
      is worth doing now and keeping automatic updates on.
  - instruction: Note that the Scribe has no lock-screen ads
    detail: >-
      Unlike the Paperwhite and basic Kindle, the Scribe is not sold in an
      ad-supported version. There is no "Special Offers" setting and nothing to
      pay to remove. The sleep screen shows a rotating set of Amazon artwork or a
      book cover.
  - instruction: Pair and check the pen
    detail: >-
      The pen is battery-free and pairs automatically the first time you touch it
      to the screen. It attaches magnetically to the right edge. If it came with
      the Premium Pen, the top end is an eraser and there is a customisable
      shortcut button on the barrel; the Basic Pen has neither. Open the
      built-in tutorial notebook to try it.
  - instruction: Set the front light and warm light
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top of the screen for quick settings. The brightness
      slider controls the white light; a second control adds a warm amber tone.
      There is also an automatic schedule.
---

The Scribe sets up like any Kindle, with two differences: there are no
lock-screen ads to think about, and there is a pen to pair, which happens
automatically the first time you write. Registration connects the device to your
Amazon account, your purchases and — importantly for the Scribe — notebook sync
to the Kindle apps.
