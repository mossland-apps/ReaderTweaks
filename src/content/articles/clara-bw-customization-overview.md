Everything in this section uses the software the Clara BW shipped with. None of
it requires KOReader, and the changes are just files you copy onto the device or
settings you toggle.

## What you can change

- **Fonts.** Kobo includes a good set, and you can add your own TrueType or
  OpenType fonts by copying them to a folder on the device. On a 6-inch screen a
  slightly narrower font can gain you a useful amount of text per line.
- **The sleep and power-off screens.** Replace the default images with your own.
- **Reading layout.** Margins, line spacing, justification and font weight, all
  in the reading menu — worth tuning carefully on a small screen.
- **The light.** Brightness, warm-light level, and an automatic schedule tied to
  sunset.
