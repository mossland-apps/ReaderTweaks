  - instruction: Decide between a book cover and your own images
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Personalize", the InkPad 4 can show the cover of the

  - instruction: Prepare your images
    detail: >-

  - instruction: Connect the device and add the images
    detail: >-

  - instruction: To go back to the default
    detail: >-
      In the same Personalize menu, set the Logo and Power-off Logo back to
      "Standard" or "Book cover". The built-in artwork returns.

PocketBook makes custom sleep screens a normal setting — no developer menu, no
tapping a version number seven times like on a Kobo. Copy an image into the
"system/logo" folder, then choose it under Personalize. The InkPad 4 keeps the
sleep screen and the fully-off screen as two separate settings, so you can use a
different image for each.
