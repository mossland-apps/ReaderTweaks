Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order and copy books to a reader. With the Sage it works over a USB-C cable out
of the box, and wirelessly once you add a plugin.

## What to know before you start

- **The Sage reads EPUB directly.** You do not need to convert anything for a
  normal EPUB.
- **KEPUB is Kobo's own version of EPUB**, with better in-book page numbers and
  reading statistics on Kobo hardware. Calibre can send books as KEPUB
  automatically once you turn that setting on — it is optional.
- **PDFs go across as PDFs.** The 8-inch screen handles a normal PDF page well;
  do not convert PDFs for the Sage.
- **Sideloaded books sit next to store books** in "My Books".
- **Keep your originals as EPUB.** Convert only when needed, and keep the EPUB.
