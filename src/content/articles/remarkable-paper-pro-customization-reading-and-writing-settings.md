steps:
  - instruction: Set your writing hand and toolbar side
    detail: >-
      In Settings, then "General" (or "Stylus" / "Handedness"), choose left or
      right. This moves the floating toolbar to the other edge and tunes palm
      rejection so resting your hand does not leave marks. Left-handed users
      should set this first — the defaults assume right.
  - instruction: Adjust palm rejection if you still get stray marks
    detail: >-
      If the side of your hand still draws, make sure handedness is correct, and
      keep the Marker tip on the screen before you rest your palm — starting the
      stroke tells the device which input to trust.
  - instruction: Turn page-turn taps on or off
    detail: >-
      In the reading settings you can enable tapping the left and right edges of
      the screen to turn pages, or turn that off if you find you trigger it while
      writing near the edge. Swiping always works regardless.
  - instruction: Set a default pen and thickness
    detail: >-
      Pick the pen you use most — many people use the fineliner or ballpoint —
      and set its default thickness and colour. New notebooks then open with that
      selected instead of you choosing every time.
  - instruction: Assign the Marker Plus shortcut button
    detail: >-
      If you have the Marker Plus, its barrel button has a shortcut. In Settings,
      then "Stylus", set it to "eraser" (hold to erase, release to return to the
      pen) or to cycle the last two tools. The eraser option is the popular
      choice.
  - instruction: Control how PDFs fit the screen
    detail: >-
      Open a PDF, open its view menu, and set the fit — "fit width", "fit page",
      or a custom zoom. For a document with wide margins, zoom in and pan, or
      prepare a margin-trimmed copy in Calibre so it fills the page without
      manual zoom.
  - instruction: Set the front light for the room
    detail: >-
      Swipe down for the light controls. The Paper Pro's front light has a warm
      and a cool component; most people run it low and warm indoors and off in
      good daylight to save battery.
---

None of these are hidden or risky — they are the normal settings that decide
whether the Paper Pro feels right. Set your handedness and toolbar side first,
then a default pen, then the Marker button, and finally spend a minute per PDF
getting the fit and zoom comfortable.
