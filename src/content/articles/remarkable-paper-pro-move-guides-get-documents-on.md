steps:
  - instruction: Understand where documents live
    detail: >-
      Everything on the Move syncs through your reMarkable account. You add a
      document in one place — the desktop app, the mobile app, email or a linked
      cloud folder — and it appears on the device after a sync over Wi-Fi. There
      is no plain USB drive to drag files onto.
  - instruction: Method A — the desktop app
    detail: >-
      Open the reMarkable app on your computer, sign in, and drag PDF or EPUB
      files into the library, or use "Import". They upload to your account and
      download to the Move on its next sync. This is the main method for a batch
      of PDFs.
  - instruction: Method B — the mobile app and share sheet
    detail: >-
      On your phone, open a PDF and use "Share", then the reMarkable app, or add
      it inside the app. Good for a document that arrived by email on your phone —
      and a natural fit for a device you carry like a phone.
  - instruction: Method C — email to your device
    detail: >-
      With a reMarkable account you can email documents to a personal address
      shown in the account settings. Attachments are added to your library.
      Availability of this depends on your plan.
  - instruction: Method D — cloud integrations
    detail: >-
      With a reMarkable Connect subscription you can link Google Drive, Dropbox or
      OneNote and open files from them directly on the device. Without the
      subscription these integrations are limited or unavailable.
  - instruction: Prepare PDFs for the smaller page
    detail: >-
      A 7.3-inch screen shows a full A4 or US-Letter PDF page small. Before
      sending a text-heavy PDF, trim its margins (Calibre's "Polish books" or a
      dedicated tool) so the text fills more of the page, or convert it to a
      PDF at a page size near the Move's screen. This matters more here than on
      the bigger reMarkables.
  - instruction: Know the EPUB limitation
    detail: >-
      The Move opens EPUB, but its handling is basic — limited font and layout
      control, no real library organisation, and some EPUBs render poorly. For
      serious ebook reading, a Kobo or Kindle is far better. Use the Move for
      PDFs and for the occasional EPUB you also want to mark up.
---

The Move has no USB file mode — documents come in through the reMarkable apps,
email, or cloud links, and sync over Wi-Fi. The mobile app suits it best, since
it is a device you carry. The one extra habit worth forming is trimming or
resizing PDFs before you send them: a full-size page is cramped on a 7.3-inch
screen, and a minute of preparation makes a document properly readable.
