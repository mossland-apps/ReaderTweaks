The Colorsoft is a locked-down device, so these guides are mostly about the
things you do around it rather than on it, plus one guide on what the colour
screen is actually good for.
[Calibre](/devices/kindle/colorsoft/calibre/) covers library management on a
computer; [Customization](/devices/kindle/colorsoft/customization/) covers fonts
and ads; [KOReader](/devices/kindle/colorsoft/koreader/) is the alternative
reading app and its trade-offs.

## Start here

  — registration, Wi-Fi, the light settings and the colour options.
- **[Get your own books onto the Kindle Colorsoft](/devices/kindle/colorsoft/guides/send-to-kindle/)**
  — the Send to Kindle service by email, app and browser.
- **[Get the most from the Colorsoft's colour screen](/devices/kindle/colorsoft/guides/colour-screen/)**
  — covers, highlights, comics and PDFs, and what to expect.
