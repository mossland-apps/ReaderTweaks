The Page runs Android, so KOReader is just an app. You install it from the Google
Play Store or by sideloading the Android APK — there is no jailbreak, no launcher
to register, and the stock BOOX software is untouched.

## Why put it on a BOOX

The BOOX stock reader (NeoReader) is capable, and the Kindle and Kobo apps cover
store books, so the reasons to add KOReader are specific:

- **Typography and layout control** beyond what NeoReader or the store apps
  expose — fine margin, hyphenation and font-weight settings.
- **Format range** — CBZ, CBR, FB2, DjVu and a genuinely usable PDF reflow, all
  in one app.
- **Its own refresh handling** — KOReader manages e-ink refresh itself and often
  looks cleaner than a generic app forced through BOOX's optimisation.
- **Configurable buttons** — the Page's physical page-turn buttons, and every
  gesture, can be assigned an action inside KOReader.
- **Consistency** — the same reading app and settings as your other devices,
  with a synced position.

## What to know

- KOReader will not open DRM-protected store books (Kindle, Kobo purchases,
  library loans). Use the store apps for those.
- After installing, set KOReader's BOOX per-app refresh mode to HD or Regal, and
  let KOReader control full refresh from inside its own settings.
