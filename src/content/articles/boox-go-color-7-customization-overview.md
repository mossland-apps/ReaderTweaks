Because it is a full Android device, the Go Color 7 is the most configurable
reader on this site. The catch is that "customization" here means Android
settings and BOOX's own layers on top, not a folder of files.

## What you can change

- **Fonts** in the stock NeoReader app, and in most third-party readers, by
  adding font files to a folder.
- **The Navigation Ball** — a floating on-screen button with shortcuts to
  refresh, back, volume and app switching — and edge-swipe gestures.
- **Per-app display behaviour** — the optimisation dialog that sets refresh mode,
  contrast, DPI and animation handling for each app.
- **Buttons** — remap the two physical page-turn buttons and their long-press
  actions.
- **The launcher and home screen** — BOOX's own, or a third-party e-ink launcher
  from the Play Store.
