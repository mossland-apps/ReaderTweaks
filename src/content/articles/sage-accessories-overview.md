The Sage is large and a little heavy, so a case that doubles as a stand earns its
place. The Kobo Stylus is only worth it if you plan to take notes. The Power
Cover adds a second battery and a lot of bulk and cost, and most people do not
need it. Picks are grouped by type below, with a note on which are cut for this
device and which fit any reader.
