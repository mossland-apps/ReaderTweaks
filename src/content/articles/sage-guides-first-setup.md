steps:
  - instruction: Charge the Sage before the first setup
    detail: >-
      A new device often ships with a low battery, and setup includes a software
      update that should not be interrupted. Plug it in with the supplied USB-C
      cable and give it 30 minutes or more before you start. If you also bought
      the Power Cover, charge that separately later.
  - instruction: Turn it on and choose your language
    detail: >-
      Press the power button on the top edge for a couple of seconds. The first
      screen is a language list; this sets the menu language, not the language of
      your books.
  - instruction: Connect to Wi-Fi
    detail: >-
      Pick your network and enter its password. The Sage needs this once to
      activate and download its current firmware. After setup you can leave Wi-Fi
      off to save battery and turn it on only when you want to sync or shop.
  - instruction: Let the firmware update finish
    detail: >-
      The device downloads and installs the latest Kobo software, shows a
      progress bar, and restarts once. This can take five to ten minutes. Leave
      it plugged in and do not press anything until it returns to the setup
      screens.
  - instruction: Decide whether to sign in with a Kobo account
    detail: >-
      A Kobo account is only needed to buy or borrow books through the Kobo store
      and the built-in OverDrive library feature. It is not required to read your
      own EPUB files. You can sign in with an existing Kobo, Google or Apple
      account, or look for the option to skip and set the device up without one.
    tip: >-
      If you mainly plan to sideload books with Calibre or use KOReader, skipping
      the account keeps setup simple. You can always sign in later from Settings.
  - instruction: Set the front light and warm light to taste
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top of the screen to open the quick settings. The
      brightness slider controls the white front light; the second slider adds a
      warm amber tone. There is also a schedule that shifts it automatically
      after sunset.
  - instruction: Pair the Kobo Stylus if you have one
    detail: >-
      The Sage works with the original Kobo Stylus and the Kobo Stylus 2. Hold
      the stylus button while the Sage prompts you, or open Settings, then
      "Stylus", to pair. The stylus needs a AAAA battery (original) or a USB-C
      charge (Stylus 2).
  - instruction: Add your books
    detail: >-
      Connect the Sage to a computer with the USB-C cable and copy EPUB files
      into any folder on the drive, or use Calibre. On the home screen, open "My
      Books" to see them.

Setting up a Sage is short, and the only decision that trips people up is the
account step. Nothing about the account is mandatory for reading books you
already own — it exists for the Kobo store, reading-stats sync, and borrowing
from a public library. If none of those matter to you, set the device up without
an account and move straight to adding your own books.
