There is not much to change on a Kindle. The Scribe has no custom sleep images,
no gesture remapping, and no third-party apps. What you can do:

- **Add your own fonts** for reading, from a "fonts" folder on the device — the
  same as any current Kindle.
- **Choose notebook templates** from the built-in set (lined, dotted, grid,
  planner and more), per notebook or per page. You cannot add your own.
- **Set the Premium Pen shortcut button** to the eraser or the last-used tool,
  under Settings, then Pen.
- **Adjust the reading layout** — font size, boldness, spacing, margins — in the
  reading menu.
- **Turn off features you find distracting**, such as Popular Highlights or the
  AI notebook summary, in Settings.

Running KOReader, which is how you would get real typography control on a Kobo,
[is not a practical option on the Scribe](/devices/kindle/scribe/koreader/).
