steps:
  - instruction: Decide which apps you actually need
    detail: >-
      The Note Air 4 C can run all of them, but each app you sign into is another
      account and another thing syncing. Common picks are the Kindle app (Amazon
      purchases), the Kobo app (Kobo store and library books), Libby (public
      library loans), and a general reader like Moon+ Reader or ReadEra for your
      own files. KOReader has its own section.
  - instruction: Install from the Play Store
    detail: >-
      Open the Play Store, search for the app, and install it as you would on a
      tablet. If an app is missing from search, it may be filtered for "large
      screen" or region — try the Play Store website in the device's browser, or
      sideload the APK from a trusted source such as the developer's site.
  - instruction: Open the app once, then open BOOX's per-app settings
    detail: >-
      Launch the app so Android registers it. Then go to the Apps screen, find
      the app, and open its BOOX "optimisation" panel — either a long-press
      option, or Settings, then "App optimisation". This dialog is where you fix
      how the app looks and behaves.
  - instruction: Set the display and refresh options per app
    detail: >-
      For a text reading app choose a clean refresh mode (Regal or Normal), turn
      on "bleach" or "enhance contrast" only if text looks grey, and enable "full
      refresh on page turn" if you see heavy ghosting. For a comic or magazine
      app you want colour, leave it in colour mode and allow a slightly faster
      refresh. Getting this right per app is the single biggest quality
      difference on a BOOX.
  - instruction: Control background behaviour
    detail: >-
      In the same area, or under Settings, then "Power", set reading apps you
      only open occasionally to "optimised" or "restricted" so they do not drain
      the battery syncing. Keep an app you use constantly exempt so it resumes
      instantly.
  - instruction: Sign in and turn off in-app animations where you can
    detail: >-
      Sign into each app's account. In the app's own settings, turn off page-turn
      animations, "curl" effects and auto-brightness — they look bad and stutter
      on e-ink. Set the app's own theme to a plain light mode.
---

The Note Air 4 C's strength is that it runs the real Kindle, Kobo, Libby and
other apps alongside its note-taking, so you are not locked to one store. The
price is that each app needs two rounds of setup: the app's own account and
settings, and BOOX's per-app optimisation panel that controls refresh and
background behaviour. Spend the ten minutes per app — it is the difference
between a BOOX that feels good and one that feels broken.
