KOReader is a separate reading application that runs on the Libra Colour without
removing anything. When you install it, the Kobo home screen gains a "KOReader"
icon; tapping it starts KOReader, and closing KOReader returns you to the normal
Kobo interface (often called **Nickel**, the name of Kobo's own software).

## Why put it on the Libra Colour

- **Typography.** KOReader exposes margin, line-spacing, font-weight and
  hyphenation controls that Nickel keeps hidden. On a 7-inch screen that is the
  difference between a comfortable page and a cramped one.
- **Formats.** It opens CBZ and CBR comic archives, FB2, DjVu and plain-text
  files that Nickel either handles poorly or not at all.
- **The colour screen.** KOReader's image handling and its comic-book mode make
  better use of the Libra Colour's colour panel than the stock reader does.
- **One setup everywhere.** If you also read on a phone or another e-reader,
  KOReader can be the same on all of them, including a synced reading position.

## Why you might leave it off

- Nickel's Kobo store, OverDrive library borrowing and Pocket integration do not
  work inside KOReader. If you rely on those, you will be switching back and
  forth.
- The interface is dense. Expect to spend an evening learning where things are.
