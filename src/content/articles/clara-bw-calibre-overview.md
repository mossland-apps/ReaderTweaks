Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order and copy books onto a reader. With the Clara BW it works over a USB-C
cable out of the box, and wirelessly once you add a plugin.

## What to know before you start

- **The Clara BW reads EPUB directly.** You do not need to convert anything for
  a normal EPUB to work.
- **KEPUB is Kobo's own version of EPUB.** It adds better in-book page numbers
  and reading statistics on Kobo devices. Calibre can send books as KEPUB
  automatically once you turn that setting on — it is optional, and covered as
  an aside in the guide below.
- **Sideloaded books sit next to store books** in "My Books" on the home screen.
- **Keep your originals as EPUB.** Convert only when a specific device needs it,
  and keep the EPUB. Every conversion can introduce small formatting errors.

The Clara BW's 6-inch screen means margins and font size matter more than on a
larger reader; that is a setting inside the book, not something Calibre controls.
