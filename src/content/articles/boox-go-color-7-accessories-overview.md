The Go Color 7 needs a case, like any reader that goes in a bag. Beyond that, a
microSD card is genuinely useful here: it lets you keep a large sideloaded
library separate from app data, and it survives a factory reset. Bluetooth
earbuds are worth it if you play audiobooks or use text-to-speech in an app.
Picks are grouped by type below, with a note on which are cut for this device and
which fit any reader.
