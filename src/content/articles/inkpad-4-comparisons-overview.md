The InkPad 4 is PocketBook's large black-and-white reader. Its case is the
7.8-inch, 300-ppi screen: full PDFs, comics, sheet music and textbooks without
constant zooming, plus buttons, IPX8 waterproofing, a built-in speaker with
Bluetooth, and open format support.

- **PocketBook InkPad Color 3.** The same body and features with an E Ink
  Kaleido 3 colour layer. Colour comes at a cost — the black-and-white text is a
  touch less crisp and the colour itself is muted and lower-resolution. Choose
  the Color 3 if you read colour comics, magazines or illustrated non-fiction;
  choose the InkPad 4 if you want the sharpest possible text and a slightly
  lower price.
- **PocketBook Era.** The same software and audio in a 7-inch body that is
  smaller, lighter and easier to hold one-handed, but with less room for a
  full-size PDF page. Choose the Era for novels and portability; choose the
  InkPad 4 when documents are the main job.
- **Kobo Libra Colour.** A 7-inch Kobo with buttons, waterproofing, colour and a
  stylus, but a closed format list, no speaker, and a smaller screen for PDFs.
  Choose the Libra Colour for Kobo's store and note-taking; choose the InkPad 4
  for the big screen and format freedom.
