Everything in this section uses the software the Libra Colour shipped with. None
of it requires KOReader, and none of it voids anything — the changes are files
you copy onto the device or settings you toggle.

## What you can change

- **Fonts.** Kobo includes a good set, and you can add your own TrueType or
  OpenType fonts by copying them to a folder on the device.
- **The sleep and power-off screens.** Replace the default images with your own.
- **Reading layout.** Margins, line spacing, justification and font weight, all
  in the reading menu.
- **The light.** Brightness, warm-light level, and an automatic schedule tied to
  sunset.
