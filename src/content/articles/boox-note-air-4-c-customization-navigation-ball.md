steps:
  - instruction: Turn on the Navigation Ball
    detail: >-
      Open Settings, then look for "Navigation Ball" (sometimes under "Function
      settings" or the control centre). Enable it. A small circular button
      appears floating over whatever app you are in; drag it to a corner within
      thumb reach.
  - instruction: Choose what its shortcuts do
    detail: >-
      Tapping the ball fans out a ring of shortcuts. Set them to the actions you
      reach for most — full-screen refresh (clears ghosting), Back, Home, recent
      apps, a colour/black-and-white toggle, and a brightness or refresh-mode
      control. You usually get four to nine slots.
  - instruction: Set the full-refresh shortcut first
    detail: >-
      The single most useful shortcut is "refresh" or "full-screen refresh".
      Ghosting builds up in any app and is more visible on the colour screen; a
      one-tap clear from the ball beats opening the control centre every time.
  - instruction: Set up edge-swipe gestures
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Gesture" (or "Function settings"), assign swipes from
      the left, right and bottom edges. On a 10.3-inch screen these matter more
      than on a small reader — swipe up from the bottom for recent apps or the
      control centre, swipe from the left edge for Back, so you are not stretching
      to on-screen buttons.
  - instruction: Keep gestures clear of the writing area
    detail: >-
      In BOOX Notes and any drawing app, edge swipes can be mistaken for pen
      strokes near the border. Either keep a margin, or use the app's palm-
      rejection and "disable gestures while writing" option.
  - instruction: Hide the ball when writing or in full-screen apps
    detail: >-
      The ball gets in the way in a note app or browser. It has an
      auto-hide/opacity option, and you can toggle it off quickly from the
      control centre when you need the whole screen.
---

The Navigation Ball turns a menu dive into one tap, and on a screen this size
its best use is a fast full-screen refresh plus Back and Home so you are not
reaching across the glass. Set that up first, configure the edge swipes for
moving around Android, and make sure neither interferes with the pen when you
are writing near the edge.
