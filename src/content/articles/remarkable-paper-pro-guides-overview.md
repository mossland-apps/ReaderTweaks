The Paper Pro is a writing tablet, so these guides are about documents and notes
rather than an ebook library. This section covers setting up a new one, the
several ways to get PDFs and EPUBs onto it, and how the notebooks and handwriting
conversion work. [Calibre](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pro/calibre/) and
[KOReader](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pro/koreader/) have their own
sections; [Customization](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pro/customization/)
covers templates and writing settings.

  — the account, the Connect subscription question, and pairing the Marker.
- **[Get documents onto the reMarkable Paper Pro](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pro/guides/get-documents-on/)**

  awkward.
