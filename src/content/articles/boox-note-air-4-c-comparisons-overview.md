The Note Air 4 C's case is that it does two jobs on one device: a colour e-ink
reader that runs every store's app, and a large pen tablet for notes and PDF
markup. The trade-off is setup — refresh modes, per-app optimisation, and a
colour layer that mutes the screen — and shorter standby than a single-purpose
device.

- **Kindle Scribe.** A bigger, black-and-white, locked Amazon device with the
  best-polished note experience and superb battery life, but no apps beyond
  Amazon's and export only by email. Choose the Scribe if you live in the Kindle
  store and want writing to just work; choose the Note Air 4 C if you need
  colour, other apps, or files you can copy off over USB.
- **reMarkable Paper Pro.** A colour e-ink writing tablet with the best pen feel
  and a clean, distraction-free design, but a deliberately closed system with a
  subscription for some cloud features and very limited reading apps. Choose the
  reMarkable if writing is the whole point; choose the Note Air 4 C if reading
  apps matter as much as the pen.
- **BOOX Go Color 7.** The same BOOX Android software and colour e-ink in a small
  7-inch reader with no pen. Choose it if you want a pocketable colour reader and
  do not need note-taking; the Note Air 4 C is the choice when you want the big
  screen and the stylus.
