steps:
  - instruction: Open the optimisation dialog for an app
    detail: >-
      From the Apps screen, long-press an app and choose "Optimise" (or open the
      app, then swipe down for the control centre and tap the optimisation icon).
      A panel of sliders and toggles appears, applying only to that app.
  - instruction: Set the refresh mode
    detail: >-
      Text readers get HD or Regal; browsers and stores get Balanced or Fast.
      This overrides the global mode whenever the app is in front.
  - instruction: Set full refresh frequency
    detail: >-
      Every 1 page is cleanest but flickers most; every 3 to 5 is a common
      compromise. For an app that manages its own refresh (KOReader), turn this
      off.
  - instruction: Adjust contrast, bleach and sharpen
    detail: >-
      "Enhance contrast" or "bleach whites" fixes grey-looking text. Use it
      sparingly — too much crushes thin fonts. "Sharpen" or "bold text" can help
      small type, which matters on the 6-inch screen.
  - instruction: Fix the DPI so phone apps are not cramped
    detail: >-
      Many apps show a tiny phone-sized layout on the small screen. The DPI
      slider rescales the app — nudge it until buttons and text are comfortable,
      then reopen the app to apply. This is one of the most useful settings on a
      6-inch BOOX.
  - instruction: Filter animations
    detail: >-
      "Animation filter" stops page-curl effects, fade-ins and spinners that
      stutter on e-ink. Turn it on for almost every reading and store app.
  - instruction: Handle dark themes
    detail: >-
      If an app forces a dark theme, use the optimisation dialog's colour-invert
      or "force light" option so text is dark-on-light.
---

The optimisation dialog is where a BOOX goes from "usable" to "good". On the
small Go 6 screen, the DPI setting is especially worth your time — many Android
apps assume a phone and look cramped until you rescale them. Otherwise: pick a
refresh mode, set full-refresh frequency (off for KOReader), nudge contrast only
if text is grey, and filter animations.
