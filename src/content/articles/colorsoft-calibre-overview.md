Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order and copy books onto a reader. With a Colorsoft it works over USB out of
the box, and it can also send books wirelessly to your Kindle email address.

## What to know before you start

- **Send AZW3, not EPUB.** The Colorsoft does not read EPUB files copied over by
  cable. Calibre converts to AZW3 (also called KF8) automatically when it sends
  to a Kindle, and keeps your EPUB copy in its library.
- **Colour pictures survive.** Images inside an EPUB book stay in colour through
  the conversion and show in colour on the device. Comics are the exception:
  Calibre's comic conversion turns pages grey unless you tick "Disable
  conversion of images to grayscale".
- **KFX is optional.** Amazon's newer format gives slightly better typography.
  Calibre can produce it only with the community "KFX Output" plugin. AZW3 is
  fine for most reading.
- **Comics need a separate tool.** Calibre can store comic files, but for good
  results on a Kindle most people convert them with Kindle Comic Converter
  first. See the
  [colour-screen guide](/devices/kindle/colorsoft/guides/colour-screen/).
- **Keep your originals as EPUB.** Convert once, from the best source, and keep
  the EPUB.
