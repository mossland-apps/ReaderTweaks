The Palma 2 runs Android, so KOReader is just an app. You install it from the
Google Play Store or by sideloading the Android APK — no jailbreak, and the
stock BOOX software is untouched.

## Why put it on a Palma 2

- **Typography and layout control** — on a 6.13-inch screen the margin,
  hyphenation and font-weight settings matter a lot, and KOReader exposes all of
  them.
- **Format range** — CBZ, CBR, FB2, DjVu and a usable PDF reflow, in one app.
- **Its own refresh handling** — often cleaner than a generic app forced through
  BOOX's optimisation.
- **Volume-key and gesture control** — KOReader can map the Palma 2's volume keys
  to page turns and assign every corner and swipe.
- **Consistency** — the same reading app and synced position as your other
  devices.

## What to know

- KOReader will not open DRM-protected store books (Kindle, Kobo, library loans).
  Use the store apps for those.
- After installing, set KOReader's BOOX per-app refresh mode to HD or Regal, and
  let KOReader control full refresh from inside its own settings.
