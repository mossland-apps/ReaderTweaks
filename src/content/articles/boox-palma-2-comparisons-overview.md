The Palma 2 is the odd one out: a phone-shaped black-and-white e-ink device with
no SIM slot, a camera, a speaker and full Android with the Play Store. It is
built to slip into a pocket and be read one-handed like a phone.

- **BOOX Go 6.** The closest match on screen and software — same black-and-white
  e-ink, same Android, same microSD slot — but in a traditional reader shape
  that needs two hands or a stand. Pick the Go 6 if you read sitting down and
  want a larger, more book-like page; pick the Palma 2 to read standing on a
  train with one hand.
- **Kobo Clara BW.** A dedicated 6-inch Kobo, not Android. It works out of the
  box, lasts far longer on a charge, and has a more polished store — but it
  cannot run the Kindle app, a browser, or feed readers. Choose the Clara BW for
  a calm single-purpose reader; choose the Palma 2 to carry your books, articles
  and audiobooks on one pocket device.
- **BOOX Go Color 7.** Bigger, colour, with page-turn buttons. It is a better
  main reader; the Palma 2 is a better carry-everywhere second device.
