The entry Kindle is a locked-down device, so these guides are mostly about the
things you do around it. This section covers setting up a new one and loading
your own EPUB books. [Calibre](/devices/kindle/kindle-basic/calibre/) covers
library management on a computer;
[Customization](/devices/kindle/kindle-basic/customization/) covers ads and
fonts; [KOReader](/devices/kindle/kindle-basic/koreader/) is the alternative
reading app and its trade-offs.
