The Note Air 4 C is large, comes with its pen, and is a device you carry to
meetings, so protection and keeping the pen attached matter most. A folio with a
pen holder is the first purchase — the pen's magnetic grip on the bare edge is
weak. Replacement pen tips are a consumable you will need eventually. A microSD
card keeps a large sideloaded library and PDF collection separate from app data
and safe through a factory reset. Bluetooth earbuds are the way to hear
audiobooks or text-to-speech away from the speaker. Picks are grouped by type
below.
