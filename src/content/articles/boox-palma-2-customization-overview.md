Because it is a full Android device, the Palma 2 is highly configurable. The
catch is that "customization" here means Android settings and BOOX's own layers
on top, not a folder of files.

## What you can change

- **Fonts** in the stock NeoReader app, and in most third-party readers, by
  adding font files to a folder.
- **The volume keys and gestures** — map the volume rocker to page turns, and set
  the Navigation Ball and edge swipes for one-handed use.
- **Per-app display behaviour** — the optimisation dialog that sets refresh mode,
  contrast, DPI and animation handling for each app. On the small screen, DPI is
  the setting you will use most.
- **The launcher and home screen** — BOOX's own, or a third-party e-ink launcher
  from the Play Store.
