Because it is a full Android device, the Go 6 is highly configurable. The catch
is that "customization" here means Android settings and BOOX's own layers on
top, not a folder of files.

## What you can change

- **Fonts** in the stock NeoReader app, and in most third-party readers, by
  adding font files to a folder.
- **Gestures and the Navigation Ball** — since the Go 6 has no buttons, the
  floating Navigation Ball and edge-swipe gestures do the work of hardware keys.
- **Per-app display behaviour** — the optimisation dialog that sets refresh mode,
  contrast, DPI and animation handling for each app.
- **The launcher and home screen** — BOOX's own, or a third-party e-ink launcher
  from the Play Store.
