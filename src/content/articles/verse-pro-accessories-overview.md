The Verse Pro is compact and well-built, and needs little. A folio case that
sleeps and wakes the device is the one purchase most people should make. If you
use the text-to-speech or play audiobook files, a set of Bluetooth earbuds is
the other thing worth having, since the Verse Pro has no speaker. Picks are
grouped by type below, with a note on which are cut for this device and which
fit any reader.
