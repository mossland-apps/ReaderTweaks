steps:
  - instruction: Understand what makes the Verse different
    detail: >-
      Unlike a Kobo or Kindle, the Verse opens most formats directly — EPUB, PDF,
      FB2, DjVu, DOCX, RTF, TXT, HTML, CBZ, CBR, MOBI and others. You almost never
      need to convert a file first. That makes copying books on the simplest
      method.
  - instruction: Method A — copy by USB cable
    detail: >-
      Connect the Verse to a computer with the USB-C cable and choose the
      connection prompt. It appears as a drive. Drag your book files into any
      folder, or into the "Books" folder to keep things tidy. Eject the drive
      safely and unplug; the device indexes the new files and shows them in the
      Library.
  - instruction: Method B — use a microSD card
    detail: >-
      Copy books onto a microSD card on your computer, power the Verse off, and
      insert the card. Its contents appear in the Library alongside internal
      books. This keeps a large sideloaded library separate and safe through a
      device reset.
  - instruction: Method C — Send-to-PocketBook by email
    detail: >-
      With a PocketBook account, the device has its own address such as
      "yourname@pbsync.com" (find it under Settings, then Accounts and
      Synchronization, then Send-to-PocketBook). Email your book files as
      attachments to that address. On the Verse, open the "Send-to-PocketBook"
      folder and sync; the files download over Wi-Fi.
    tip: >-
      This is the method to use from a phone, or to have another program deliver
      books for you.
  - instruction: Method D — sync a cloud folder
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then Accounts and Synchronization, you can link Dropbox or
      Google Drive, or use the PocketBook Cloud. Put books in the linked folder
      from any computer, then sync on the device to pull them down. The
      PocketBook Cloud also keeps your reading position in step with the
      PocketBook phone app.
  - instruction: Open a book and check the layout
    detail: >-
      For EPUB and similar reflowable formats, the reading menu (tap the top
      centre) has font, spacing and margin controls. For PDFs, look for the
      "Reflow" or "Mode" option in that menu, which re-flows the text to fit the
      6-inch screen — fixed PDF pages are hard to read at this size otherwise.
---

The Verse's format range means "getting books on" is usually just copying a
file. Use the USB or microSD method for a batch, Send-to-PocketBook for the odd
book from your phone, and cloud sync if you want a folder on your computer to
mirror the device. Conversion, the awkward part on other readers, rarely comes
up here.
