  - instruction: Decide between a book cover and your own images
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Personalize", the Verse can show the cover of the
      book you are reading on the sleep screen. This guide is for the other
      option — showing your own fixed images.
  - instruction: Prepare your images
    detail: >-
      The screen is 758 by 1024 pixels. Portrait images at that size look best.
      Greyscale PNG or JPEG shows you exactly how they will appear, since the
      panel has a limited range of grey.
  - instruction: Connect the device and add the images
    detail: >-
      Connect over USB and open the drive. In the "system" folder there is a
      "logo" folder (create it if missing). Copy the image you want for the sleep
      screen there, and note its file name.
  - instruction: Point the sleep screen at your image
    detail: >-
      On the Verse, open Settings, then "Personalize", then "Logo" (or "Standby
      screen"). Choose "Select image" and pick the file you copied. There is a
      matching "Power-off Logo" setting for the screen shown when the device is
      fully off.
  - instruction: Test it
    detail: >-
      Press the power button once to sleep the device and confirm your image
      appears. Power off completely to check the power-off screen.
  - instruction: To go back to the default
    detail: >-
      In the same Personalize menu, set the Logo and Power-off Logo back to
      "Standard" or "Book cover". The built-in artwork returns.

PocketBook makes custom sleep screens a normal setting — no developer menu, no
tapping a version number seven times like on a Kobo. Copy an image into the
"system/logo" folder, then choose it under Personalize. The Verse keeps the
sleep screen and the fully-off screen as two separate settings, so you can use a
different image for each.
