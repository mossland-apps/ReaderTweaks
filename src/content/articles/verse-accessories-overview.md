The Verse is compact and needs little. A folio case that sleeps and wakes the
device on open and close is the one purchase most people should make. A microSD
card is the other genuinely useful item here: the Verse has a slot, and a card
keeps a large sideloaded library separate from internal storage and safe through
a factory reset. The Verse has no speaker or Bluetooth, so audio accessories do
not apply. Picks are grouped by type below, with a note on which are cut for
this device and which fit any reader.
