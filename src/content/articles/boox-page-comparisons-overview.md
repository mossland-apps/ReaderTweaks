The Page is the reading-focused BOOX: a 7-inch black-and-white screen, physical
page-turn buttons, a speaker, and full Android with the Play Store. It runs any
reading app but has no colour and no waterproofing.

- **BOOX Go Color 7.** The same idea with a colour screen and a slightly
  different body. If you read comics, highlight in colour, or want the colour
  layer, pick the Go Color 7; if you only read text, the Page's black-and-white
  panel is sharper and cheaper.
- **Kobo Libra Colour.** Also 7-inch with buttons, also colour, but a dedicated
  reader rather than Android — it works out of the box, is waterproof, and has
  far better standby, at the cost of not running the Kindle app or arbitrary
  apps. Choose the Libra Colour if you want it to just work in the Kobo or
  KOReader world; choose the Page if you need the Kindle app, Libby, or apps in
  general on one device.
- **Kindle Paperwhite.** A locked 7-inch Amazon reader, waterproof, with the
  best standby of the four. Choose it only if you buy from the Kindle store and
  want simplicity. The Page can run the Kindle app and everything else besides.
