PocketBook's software is more open than Kobo's or Amazon's: you can copy almost
any book file straight onto the device and it will open. The InkPad Color 3 adds
a 7.8-inch E Ink Kaleido 3 colour screen, which changes what it is good for —
comics, magazines, illustrated non-fiction and colour-coded study notes. This
section covers setting up a new one, loading books, getting the colour screen to
look its best, and its speaker-and-Bluetooth audio. The
[Calibre](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-inkpad-color-3/calibre/) and
[KOReader](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-inkpad-color-3/koreader/) sections
cover those;
[Customization](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-inkpad-color-3/customization/)
covers fonts, remapping the buttons and the sleep screen.
