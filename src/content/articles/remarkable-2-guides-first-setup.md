steps:
  - instruction: On a used unit, confirm it is deregistered and reset
    detail: >-
      If you bought the reMarkable 2 secondhand, it should arrive factory reset
      and removed from the previous owner's account. If it boots straight into
      someone's notebooks, ask them to remove it from their account at
      my.remarkable.com, then factory reset it from Settings, then "General",
      then "Storage" (or "Reset"). A device still tied to another account cannot
      sync to yours.
  - instruction: Charge it before setup
    detail: >-
      Plug it in with a USB-C cable and give it time on the charger. The battery
      is large; a full charge takes a while, and a used unit may arrive nearly
      empty.
  - instruction: Turn it on, connect to Wi-Fi, and sign in to a reMarkable account
    detail: >-
      Press the power button on the top edge. Choose your language, connect to
      Wi-Fi, and create or sign in to a reMarkable account. An account is
      effectively required — it is how the device syncs and how the apps connect.
  - instruction: Understand the reMarkable Connect subscription
    detail: >-
      The device works without a subscription for on-device writing and reading.
      A paid "reMarkable Connect" plan adds unlimited cloud sync history, the
      mobile and desktop apps' full features, handwriting-to-text in more places,
      and integrations with Google Drive, Dropbox and OneNote. A used device may
      or may not still have a trial available. Decide later; nothing is lost by
      skipping it.
  - instruction: Let the software update install
    detail: >-
      The reMarkable 2 still receives updates even though it is no longer sold
      new. Install what is offered and let it restart — unless you plan to run
      community software such as KOReader, in which case check the community's
      guidance on which firmware to stay on first.
  - instruction: Pair and check the Marker
    detail: >-
      The Marker is battery-free and pairs the first time you touch it to the
      screen. It attaches magnetically to the right edge. The reMarkable 2 uses
      its own generation of Marker and Marker Plus — the Paper Pro's newer pens
      do not work on it, and vice versa. On a used unit, check the tip is not
      worn flat.
  - instruction: There is no front light
    detail: >-
      The reMarkable 2 has no front light. Plan to use it in ambient light, or
      add a clip-on reading light for low-light use.
  - instruction: Install the desktop and mobile apps and set handedness
    detail: >-
      Install the reMarkable app on your computer and phone and sign in with the
      same account — the desktop app is the main way to move PDFs across. In
      Settings, set your writing hand so the toolbar and palm rejection sit on
      the correct side.
---

Setting up a reMarkable 2 is like any reMarkable: an account is essentially
required, and there is an optional paid subscription (Connect) for the fuller
cloud features. The one extra step, since most reMarkable 2s sold now are used
or refurbished, is confirming the device is factory reset and removed from the
previous owner's account before you sign in.
