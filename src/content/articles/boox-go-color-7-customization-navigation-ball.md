steps:
  - instruction: Turn on the Navigation Ball
    detail: >-
      Open Settings, then look for "Navigation Ball" (sometimes under "Function
      settings" or the control centre). Enable it. A small circular button
      appears floating over whatever app you are in; drag it to a corner out of
      the way of text.
  - instruction: Choose what its shortcuts do
    detail: >-
      Tapping the ball fans out a ring of shortcuts. Set them to the actions you
      reach for most — a common set is: full-screen refresh (clears ghosting),
      Back, Home, recent apps, and a brightness or refresh-mode toggle. You
      usually get four to nine slots.
  - instruction: Set the full-refresh shortcut first
    detail: >-
      The single most useful shortcut is "refresh" or "full-screen refresh".
      Ghosting builds up in any app; a one-tap clear from the ball beats opening
      the control centre every time.
  - instruction: Set up edge-swipe gestures
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Gesture" (or "Function settings"), you can assign
      swipes from the left, right and bottom edges. Useful choices are: swipe up
      from the bottom for recent apps or the control centre, swipe from the left
      edge for Back. Keep the page-turn area of your reading apps clear of these.
  - instruction: Remap the physical buttons
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Button" (or in a reading app's own settings), set the
      two side buttons to next and previous page, and assign a long-press — a
      screen refresh or bookmark are good picks. Some apps ignore the system
      mapping and need their own; KOReader is one.
  - instruction: Hide the ball when writing or in full-screen apps
    detail: >-
      The ball can get in the way in a browser or a note app. It has an
      auto-hide/opacity option, and you can toggle it off quickly from the
      control centre when you need the whole screen.
---

The Navigation Ball turns a two- or three-step menu dive into one tap, and its
best use is a fast full-screen refresh to clear ghosting without leaving your
book. Set that up first, add Back and Home, and configure the edge swipes so you
can move around Android without hunting for on-screen buttons that are hard to
see on e-ink.
