The Paper Pro is the most focused writing device here: a large screen, colour, a
front light, the Marker, deep note tools, and open export. It is a poor ebook
reader by comparison, and some cloud features need a subscription.

- **Kindle Scribe.** The Paper Pro's direct rival. The Scribe is also a full
  Kindle, so it reads Amazon books well and its note-taking is strong. The Paper
  Pro has a bigger screen, more paper-like writing, colour, deeper note
  organisation, and open export (PDF, PNG). Choose the Scribe if you want one
  device for Kindle books and notes; choose the Paper Pro if writing is the
  point and you accept a weak reader.
- **BOOX Go Color 7.** A small Android tablet that runs any app, including good
  note apps and every reading app. Far more flexible and a much better ebook
  reader, but a smaller screen, shorter battery life, and note-taking that is
  app-dependent rather than a designed whole. Choose the BOOX for flexibility;
  choose the Paper Pro for a large, single-purpose writing surface.
- **Kobo Libra Colour.** Included as the "do you even need a big writing device"
  check. It is a proper 7-inch e-reader with light stylus notes, waterproof, in
  an open ecosystem, at a fraction of the price and weight. If your writing needs
  are light, this does both jobs adequately for far less.
