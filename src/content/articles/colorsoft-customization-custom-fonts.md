  - instruction: Gather the font files
    detail: >-
      You need files ending in ".ttf" or ".otf". A family usually comes as

  - instruction: Connect the Colorsoft to your computer

  - instruction: Open the "fonts" folder on the Kindle

    detail: >-
      Open a book, tap the top of the screen, and open the font settings (the
      "Aa" button), then the "Font" tab. Your added fonts appear below Amazon's
      built-in ones. Custom fonts only apply to books in Amazon's formats; they
      do not change how PDFs or comics look.

Kindles support user fonts without any jailbreak, and the Colorsoft is no
exception. Copy the files into a "fonts" folder, restart the Kindle, and pick
them from the reading menu. On a colour screen text is still shown at the full
300 ppi, so fonts look as crisp as on the Paperwhite.
