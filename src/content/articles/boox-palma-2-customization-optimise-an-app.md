  - instruction: Open the optimisation dialog
    detail: >-
      Swipe down the status bar and tap the app-optimisation icon while the app
      is open, or find the app in the BOOX app drawer, long-press it, and choose
      "Optimise". A panel of sliders and toggles appears.
  - instruction: Set the refresh mode
    detail: >-
      HD or Regal for reading apps. Balanced for apps with light scrolling. Fast
      or Ultrafast only for video-like content — text ghosts badly in those.
  - instruction: Adjust DPI to fit the small screen
    detail: >-
      This is the key one on the Palma 2. Many apps assume a phone and pack in
      too much. Lower the DPI a notch or two to make buttons and text bigger;
      raise it if an app wastes space. Change it in small steps and reopen the
      app to check.
  - instruction: Tune contrast and "bleach"
    detail: >-
      Use "enhance contrast" or "bleach" to darken faint grey text and clear a
      dingy background. Push it just far enough — too much crushes thin strokes.
  - instruction: Handle animations
    detail: >-
      Turn on "filter animations" or "flatten" for apps that fade and slide.
      Fewer animations means fewer half-drawn frames on e-ink.
  - instruction: Save and test
    detail: >-
      Close the dialog, fully reopen the app, and use it for a minute. Re-open
      the dialog to fine-tune. Settings are remembered per app.

Every Android app on the Palma 2 gets its own optimisation dialog — refresh
mode, contrast, DPI and animation handling. On this small a screen, DPI is the
setting that fixes the most: nudge it down to make a cramped phone app readable,
up to reclaim wasted space, and test after each change.
