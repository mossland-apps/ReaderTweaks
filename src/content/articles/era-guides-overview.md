PocketBook's software is more open than Kobo's or Amazon's: you can copy almost
any book file straight onto the device and it will open. This section covers
setting up a new Era, the several ways to load books, and its text-to-speech and
audiobook playback — which, unusually, work through a built-in speaker as well as
Bluetooth. The [Calibre](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-era/calibre/) and
[KOReader](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-era/koreader/) sections cover those;
[Customization](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-era/customization/) covers fonts,
remapping the buttons and the sleep screen.

## Start here

- **[Set up a new PocketBook Era](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-era/guides/first-setup/)**
  — accounts, Wi-Fi, and the Adobe ID for library books.
- **[Get books onto the PocketBook Era](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-era/guides/get-books-on/)**

- **[Read aloud and play audiobooks on the PocketBook Era](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-era/guides/audio-and-tts/)**
  — text-to-speech, your own audio files, the speaker and Bluetooth.
