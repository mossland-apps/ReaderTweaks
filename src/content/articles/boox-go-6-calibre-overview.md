Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order. Getting books from Calibre to the Go 6 works a few ways, because it is an
Android device rather than a dedicated reader Calibre has a driver for.

## What to know

- **Send EPUB — no conversion.** Whatever app you read in opens EPUB directly.
  There is no Kobo KEPUB or Kindle AZW3 step.
- **USB uses Android file transfer.** The Go 6 appears as an Android device
  (MTP), not a plain drive. Many people just drag files into the "Books" folder
  in the file-transfer window.
- **Wireless is often easier.** Calibre's built-in "content server" lets the Go 6
  browse and download your whole library over Wi-Fi.
- **microSD works too.** Copy books to a card on a computer, put it back, and
  every reading app can see them.
