  - instruction: Charge the Paper Pro Move before setup
    detail: >-
      A new device often ships part-charged, and setup includes a software

  - instruction: Turn it on, connect to Wi-Fi, and create a reMarkable account
    detail: >-
      Press the power button on the top edge. Choose your language, connect to
      Wi-Fi, and create or sign in to a reMarkable account. Unlike a Kindle or

  - instruction: Understand the reMarkable Connect subscription
    detail: >-
      The device works without a subscription for on-device writing and reading.
      A paid "reMarkable Connect" plan adds unlimited cloud sync history, the
      mobile and desktop apps' full features, handwriting-to-text in more places,
      and integrations with Google Drive, Dropbox and OneNote. New devices
      usually include a free trial. Decide later; nothing is lost by skipping it
      at first.
  - instruction: Let the software update install
    detail: >-
      The Move checks for and installs the latest firmware and restarts. Updates
      regularly add note-taking features and improve colour rendering, so keep
      automatic updates on unless you are running community modifications.
  - instruction: Pair and check the Marker
    detail: >-
      The Marker is battery-free and pairs the first time you touch it to the
      screen. It attaches magnetically to the edge; on the Move that magnetic
      edge also charges the current-generation Marker. If you have the Marker

  - instruction: Set the front light and handedness
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top for quick settings, including the front light. The

      toolbar and palm rejection sit on the correct side.
  - instruction: Install the desktop and mobile apps
    detail: >-
      On your computer and phone, install the reMarkable app and sign in with the
      same account. The desktop app is the main way to move PDFs onto the device;
      the mobile app is handy for sending a document from your phone and for
      reading your notes elsewhere.
  - instruction: Decide how you will carry it
    detail: >-
      The Move is pocketable, which is its whole point. A folio protects the
      screen and holds the Marker so it does not fall off in a bag. The Marker's
      magnetic hold is light — a folio loop is more secure for transport.

Setting up the Paper Pro Move is like any reMarkable, with two things that
differ from a normal e-reader: an account is essentially required, and there is
an optional paid subscription (Connect) for the fuller cloud and integration
features. The device is fully usable without it. The one setting to get right
early is the front light, since the Move is meant to be used on the go in
whatever light you are in.
