The Colorsoft is the Paperwhite with a colour screen. The real question is
whether you need colour at all, and if you do, whether you want Amazon's closed,
simple setup or a more open colour reader.

- **Kindle Paperwhite.** The same 7-inch size, body and waterproofing, with a
  black-and-white screen. It is cheaper and lasts longer on a charge (up to 12
  weeks against the Colorsoft's 8). Text looks the same on both. Choose the
  Colorsoft only if covers, highlights, comics or charts in colour matter to
  you.
- **Kobo Libra Colour.** Also 7 inches, colour and waterproof, and it adds
  physical page-turn buttons and optional pen support. Its bigger advantage is
  an open system: EPUB and comic files load directly, and KOReader installs
  without a jailbreak. Choose the Colorsoft if you buy from Amazon and want the
  simplest setup; choose the Libra Colour if you want to control your own
  library.
- **BOOX Go Color 7.** A 7-inch colour reader running Android, so it can install
  the Kindle app, Kobo app, KOReader and other reading apps side by side. More
  flexible, but more fiddly, and Android apps were not designed for e-ink.
  Choose it if you want several stores on one device.
- **Kindle Scribe Colorsoft.** Amazon's 11-inch colour model with a pen, aimed
  at note-taking and PDFs. Much bigger, heavier and more expensive. Choose it
  only if writing on the device is the point.
