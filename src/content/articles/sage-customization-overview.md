Everything in this section uses the software the Sage shipped with. None of it
requires KOReader, and the changes are files you copy onto the device or
settings you toggle.

## What you can change

- **Fonts.** Add your own TrueType or OpenType fonts by copying them to a folder
  on the device. The 8-inch screen gives fonts more room, so a wider face is
  usable here.
- **The sleep and power-off screens.** Replace the default images with your own.
- **Reading layout.** Margins, line spacing, justification and font weight, all
  in the reading menu.
- **The light.** Brightness, warm-light level, and an automatic schedule.
