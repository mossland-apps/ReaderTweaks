steps:
  - instruction: Swap or remap the page-turn buttons
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Button" (or "Key"), you can set which of the two grip
      buttons is forward and which is back — left-handed readers usually swap them
      — and assign a long-press to an action such as screen refresh, front light
      or bookmark. Some apps ignore the system mapping and need their own
      controls settings; KOReader is one.
  - instruction: Turn on the Navigation Ball
    detail: >-
      Open Settings, then "Navigation Ball" (sometimes under "Function
      settings"). Enable it. A small circular button floats over whatever app you
      are in; drag it to a corner out of the way of text.
  - instruction: Choose the Navigation Ball shortcuts
    detail: >-
      Tapping the ball fans out a ring of shortcuts. Set them to the actions you
      reach for most — a common set is: full-screen refresh, Back, Home, recent
      apps, and a brightness or refresh-mode toggle.
  - instruction: Set the full-refresh shortcut first
    detail: >-
      The single most useful shortcut is "refresh" or "full-screen refresh".
      Ghosting builds up in any app; a one-tap clear from the ball beats opening
      the control centre every time.
  - instruction: Set up edge-swipe gestures
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Gesture" (or "Function settings"), assign swipes from
      the left, right and bottom edges — for example swipe up from the bottom for
      recent apps or the control centre, swipe from the left edge for Back. Keep
      the page-turn area of your reading apps clear of these.
  - instruction: Hide the ball in full-screen apps
    detail: >-
      The ball can get in the way in a browser. It has an auto-hide and opacity
      option, and you can toggle it off quickly from the control centre when you
      need the whole screen.
---

The Page has real page-turn buttons, so the first thing to set is which one is
forward — swap them under Settings if you hold the device in your left hand.
After that, the Navigation Ball's best use is a one-tap full-screen refresh to
clear ghosting without leaving your book; set that up, add Back and Home, and
configure the edge swipes so you can move around Android without hunting for
on-screen buttons.
