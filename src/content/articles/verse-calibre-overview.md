Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order and copy books to a reader. The Verse works with it over USB out of the
box.

## What to know

- **Send EPUB — no conversion.** The Verse reads EPUB directly, so Calibre sends
  it as-is. There is no Kobo KEPUB step and no Kindle AZW3 step to worry about.
- **Calibre is optional here.** Because the device opens most formats itself, you
  can also just drag files onto the drive or a microSD card. Calibre earns its
  place if you want a tidy catalogue, bulk metadata and cover fixes, or format
  conversion for the rare file the Verse cannot open.
- **Books land wherever you send them.** Calibre copies to the drive root or the
  "Books" folder; the device indexes them on disconnect.
- **Keep your originals as EPUB.** Convert only when needed, and keep the EPUB.
