The Move is small and pocketable, and it comes with its Marker, so the
accessories that matter are protection and keeping it writing. A folio sized for
the Move protects the screen and, more importantly, holds the Marker with a loop
— the magnetic hold on the edge is light and the pen falls off in a bag.
Replacement Marker tips are a consumable you will need regularly; the Move uses
the current-generation tips, the same as the Paper Pro and Paper Pure, not the
reMarkable 2's. Picks are grouped by type below, with a note on which are cut for
this device and which fit any reader.
