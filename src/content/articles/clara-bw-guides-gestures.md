steps:
  - instruction: Learn the tap zones
    detail: >-
      While reading, a tap on the right two-thirds of the screen turns the page
      forward, a tap on the left third turns it back, and a tap across the top
      centre opens the reading menu. There are no physical buttons on this model,
      so these zones are how you turn pages by touch.
  - instruction: Use swipes as an alternative
    detail: >-
      Swipe right-to-left to go forward and left-to-right to go back. Swipes work
      anywhere on the page, which some people find more reliable than aiming for
      a tap zone one-handed.
  - instruction: Open the navigation bar and chapter list
    detail: >-
      Swipe up from the bottom edge for a bar showing your position in the book,
      a chapter list, and quick jumps. Swipe it away or tap the page to dismiss
      it.
  - instruction: Open brightness and warm light
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top edge for the quick settings panel with the
      brightness and warm-light sliders and a shortcut into Settings.
  - instruction: Adjust brightness with a slide on the left edge
    detail: >-
      Place a finger on the far left edge of the screen while reading and slide
      it up or down to change the front-light brightness without opening any
      menu. This is a built-in Kobo shortcut.
  - instruction: Change what the tap zones do
    detail: >-
      Go to the home screen, open the menu, then Settings, then "Reading
      settings". You can turn off tap-to-turn entirely and rely on swipes, or
      change which side is forward. Left-handed readers who hold the device in
      the left hand often swap the sides here.
  - instruction: Know the limit of the stock software
    detail: >-
      Kobo lets you adjust the tap zones but not assign gestures to other
      actions such as bookmarks or dictionary lookups. If you want every corner
      and swipe to be a configurable action, that is one of the reasons people
      install [KOReader](/devices/kobo/clara-bw/koreader/).
---

Because the Clara BW has no buttons, the touch controls are the whole story.
Most people settle on either tapping the sides or swiping, and adjust the tap
zones once under Reading settings if forward and back feel wrong. The left-edge
slide for brightness is worth remembering — it saves opening the menu every time
the light needs a nudge.
