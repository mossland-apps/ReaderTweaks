steps:
  - instruction: Know what the Palma 2 is
    detail: >-
      It is shaped like a phone and runs full Android 13, but it has no SIM slot
      and cannot make calls. Think of it as a pocket-sized e-ink Android device.
      Everything in setup is the same as an Android phone minus the mobile
      network.
  - instruction: Charge it, turn it on, and connect to Wi-Fi
    detail: >-
      Plug it in and give it time on the charger. Press the power button on the
      side, choose your language and time zone, then your Wi-Fi network and
      password. It needs Wi-Fi to update and to reach the Play Store.
  - instruction: Install any firmware update
    detail: >-
      Open Settings, then "Firmware Update", and apply anything offered. BOOX
      updates regularly and the updates improve how Android apps render on e-ink.
  - instruction: Decide about a BOOX account
    detail: >-
      A BOOX (Onyx) account is used for the BOOX cloud — syncing notes, reading
      position in the stock NeoReader app, and configuration backups across BOOX
      devices. It is optional; skip it if you will read mainly in the Kindle,
      Kobo or KOReader apps.
  - instruction: Turn on the Google Play Store
    detail: >-
      Open the Apps screen and find "Play Store". On first launch, BOOX shows a
      screen to "enable Google Play"; tap through it, wait a minute or two while
      it registers the device with Google, restart if prompted, and sign in with
      your Google account.
    tip: >-
      If the Play Store says "device not certified", restart the device, wait a
      few hours, and try again — Google's registration can take time to
      propagate. It almost always clears on its own.
  - instruction: Set up the fingerprint reader
    detail: >-
      The fingerprint sensor is in the power button on the side. Under Settings,
      then "Fingerprint", add one or two fingers. It unlocks the device with a
      press, which is quicker than a PIN on e-ink and natural for a phone-shaped
      device.
  - instruction: Learn the control centre and the buttons
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top-right for the BOOX control centre — refresh-mode
      buttons, front-light sliders (brightness and warm tone), and a full-screen
      refresh button. The Palma 2 has no dedicated page-turn buttons, but the
      volume rocker can be set to turn pages (covered in the one-handed guide).
  - instruction: Set a comfortable default refresh mode
    detail: >-
      In the control centre, leave the global mode on "HD" (or "Regal") for
      reading. The faster modes are for scrolling and menus and make text look
      grey. Per-app settings override this.
---

The Palma 2 sets up like an Android phone with the mobile network left out, plus
one extra step: enabling the Google Play Store, which BOOX ships switched off.
Once that is done and you have signed in to Google, it behaves like any small
Android device with an e-ink screen. Set up the fingerprint reader — on a
pocket device you will unlock it constantly.
