KOReader is a separate reading application that runs on the Clara BW without
removing anything. When you install it, the Kobo home screen gains a "KOReader"
icon; tapping it starts KOReader, and closing KOReader returns you to the normal
Kobo interface (called **Nickel**, the name of Kobo's own software).

## Why the Clara BW is a favourite for KOReader

- **It is small, light and cheap**, so it is a low-stakes device to tinker with
  and comfortable to hold for long stretches with KOReader's tighter margins.
- **The screen is current-generation**, so page turns and full refreshes are
  fast — KOReader feels responsive on it.
- **The firmware tolerates sideloading and KOReader** without much fuss, like
  the rest of the 2024 Kobo line.

## What KOReader gives you here

- **Typography control** — margins, line spacing, hyphenation and font-weight
  settings that Nickel keeps hidden, which matter more on a 6-inch page.
- **Format range** — CBZ and CBR comics, FB2, DjVu and plain text, handled
  better than in the stock reader.
- **Configurable touch zones** — every corner and swipe can be assigned an
  action, which is useful on a device with no buttons.
- **One setup across devices**, including a synced reading position.

## What you give up

Nickel's Kobo store, OverDrive borrowing, Pocket and Bluetooth audiobooks do
not work inside KOReader. If you use those, you will switch back and forth.
