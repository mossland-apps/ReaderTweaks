There is not much to change on a Kindle. Amazon does not support custom sleep
images, gesture remapping, or third-party apps. What you can do:

- **Deal with the lock-screen ads.** Remove them with a one-time payment, or
  understand what a "with Special Offers" device does and does not show.
- **Add your own fonts** for reading, from a "fonts" folder on the device.
- **Adjust the reading layout** — font size, boldness, line spacing, margins and
  alignment, in the reading menu. Note that the entry Kindle has no warm-light
  control, unlike the Paperwhite.
- **Turn off features you find distracting**, such as Popular Highlights, in
  Settings.

For anything beyond that, the honest answer is
[KOReader](/devices/kindle/kindle-basic/koreader/), which means jailbreaking.
