steps:
  - instruction: Understand what makes the InkPad Color 3 different
    detail: >-
      Unlike a Kobo or Kindle, it opens most formats directly — EPUB, PDF, FB2,
      DjVu, DOCX, RTF, TXT, HTML, CBZ, CBR, MOBI and others. You almost never
      need to convert a file first. Colour comics as CBZ or CBR, and colour PDFs,
      are exactly what this screen is for.
  - instruction: Method A — copy by USB cable
    detail: >-
      Connect the InkPad Color 3 to a computer with the USB-C cable and choose
      the connection prompt. It appears as a drive. Drag your book files into any
      folder, or into the "Books" folder to keep things tidy. Large colour comic
      archives copy faster over USB than any wireless method.
  - instruction: Method B — Send-to-PocketBook by email
    detail: >-
      With a PocketBook account, the device has its own address such as
      "yourname@pbsync.com" (find it under Settings, then Accounts and
      Synchronization, then Send-to-PocketBook). Email your book files as
      attachments to that address. On the device, open the "Send-to-PocketBook"
      folder and sync; the files download over Wi-Fi.
    tip: >-
      Email has an attachment size limit, so this suits ordinary ebooks better
      than a big colour graphic novel — use USB for those.
  - instruction: Method C — sync a cloud folder
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then Accounts and Synchronization, you can link Dropbox or
      Google Drive, or use the PocketBook Cloud. Put books in the linked folder
      from any computer, then sync on the device to pull them down.
  - instruction: Open a book and check the layout
    detail: >-
      For reflowable EPUBs, the reading menu (tap the top centre) has font,
      spacing and margin controls. For comics and colour PDFs, use the view and
      crop controls covered in the colour-screen guide so panels fill the page.

The InkPad Color 3's format range means "getting books on" is usually just
copying a file, and colour comics and magazines are what it is built to carry.
Use USB for large colour archives, Send-to-PocketBook for ordinary ebooks from
your phone, and cloud sync to mirror a folder. Conversion rarely comes up here.
