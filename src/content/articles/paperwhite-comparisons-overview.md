The Paperwhite is the middle of the Kindle range: bigger and better-equipped
than the basic Kindle, smaller and cheaper than the Scribe, and — the reason
many people cross-shop it — up against Kobo's Libra Colour.

- **Basic Kindle (11th generation).** Smaller 6-inch screen, no warm light, not
  waterproof, less storage. Genuinely fine as a plain reader, and cheaper.
  Choose the Paperwhite for the warm light, the water resistance and the larger
  page.
- **Kindle Scribe.** A 10.2-inch screen and a pen, aimed at note-taking and
  PDFs. Not waterproof, much heavier, and a lot more expensive. Choose it only
  if writing on the device matters to you.
- **Kobo Libra Colour.** Same 7-inch size and also waterproof, and it adds a
  colour screen and physical page-turn buttons. Its bigger advantage is an open
  ecosystem: sideloading EPUB and running KOReader are straightforward, where on
  the Paperwhite they mean conversion or jailbreaking. Choose the Paperwhite if
  you buy from Amazon and want the simplest possible setup; choose the Libra
  Colour if you want to control your own library.
