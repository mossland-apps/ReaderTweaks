The Paperwhite is the middle of the Kindle range: bigger and better-equipped
than the basic Kindle, smaller and cheaper than the Scribe, and — the reason
many people cross-shop it — up against Kobo's Libra Colour.

- **Basic Kindle.** Smaller 6-inch screen, no warm light, not waterproof, and
  about half the battery life (up to 6 weeks against the Paperwhite's 12).
  Genuinely fine as a plain reader, and cheaper. Choose the Paperwhite for the
  warm light, the water resistance and the larger page.
- **Kindle Scribe.** An 11-inch screen and a pen, aimed at note-taking and
  PDFs. Not waterproof, about twice the weight, and a lot more expensive. Choose
  it only if writing on the device matters to you.
- **[Kindle Colorsoft](/devices/kindle/colorsoft/).** Amazon's colour Kindle: the same 7-inch size, but a
  colour screen for covers, comics and highlights, with shorter battery life
  (up to 8 weeks) and a higher price. Choose it only if colour matters to you.
- **Kobo Libra Colour.** Same 7-inch size and also waterproof, and it adds a
  colour screen and physical page-turn buttons. Its bigger advantage is an open
  ecosystem: sideloading EPUB and running KOReader are straightforward, where on
  the Paperwhite they mean conversion or jailbreaking. Choose the Paperwhite if
  you buy from Amazon and want the simplest possible setup; choose the Libra
  Colour if you want to control your own library.
