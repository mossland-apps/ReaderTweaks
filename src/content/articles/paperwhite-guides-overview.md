The Paperwhite is a locked-down device, so these guides are mostly about the
things you do around it rather than on it. This section covers setting up a new
one and the different ways to load your own EPUB books.
[Calibre](/devices/kindle/paperwhite/calibre/) covers library management on a
computer; [Customization](/devices/kindle/paperwhite/customization/) covers ads
and fonts; [KOReader](/devices/kindle/paperwhite/koreader/) is the alternative
reading app and its trade-offs.
