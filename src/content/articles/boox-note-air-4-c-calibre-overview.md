Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order. Getting books from Calibre to the Note Air 4 C works a few ways, because
it is an Android device rather than a dedicated reader Calibre has a driver for.

## What to know

- **Send EPUB and PDF — no conversion.** Whatever app you read in (NeoReader,
  KOReader, Moon+ Reader) opens EPUB and PDF directly. There is no Kobo KEPUB or
  Kindle AZW3 step.
- **PDFs matter more here.** This is a device people buy to mark up PDFs, so
  Calibre is often just the tool that stores and tidies them before you copy
  them across.
- **USB uses Android file transfer.** The device appears as an Android device
  (MTP), not a plain drive. Many people just drag files into the "Books" folder
  in the file-transfer window.
- **Wireless is often easier.** Calibre's built-in "content server" lets the
  device browse and download your whole library over Wi-Fi.
- **microSD works too.** Copy books to a card on a computer, put it back, and
  every reading app can see them.
