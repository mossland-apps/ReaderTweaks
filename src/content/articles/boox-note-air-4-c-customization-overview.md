Because it is a full Android device, the Note Air 4 C is highly configurable.
The catch is that "customization" here means Android settings and BOOX's own
layers on top, not a folder of files.

## What you can change

- **Fonts** in the stock NeoReader app, and in most third-party readers, by
  adding font files to a folder.
- **The Navigation Ball** — a floating on-screen button with shortcuts to
  refresh, back, volume and app switching — and edge-swipe gestures.
- **Per-app display behaviour** — the optimisation dialog that sets refresh mode,
  colour versus black-and-white, contrast, DPI and animation handling for each
  app.
- **Note templates** — the page style behind every notebook, chosen per notebook
  or per page from a large built-in set.
- **The pen** — pressure, the side-button action, and calibration.
- **The launcher and home screen** — BOOX's own, or a third-party e-ink launcher
  from the Play Store.
