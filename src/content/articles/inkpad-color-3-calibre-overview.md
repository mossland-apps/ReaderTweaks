Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order and copy books to a reader. The InkPad Color 3 works with it over USB out
of the box.

## What to know

- **Send EPUB — no conversion.** The InkPad Color 3 reads EPUB directly, so
  Calibre sends it as-is. There is no Kobo KEPUB step and no Kindle AZW3 step.
- **Calibre is optional here.** The device opens most formats itself, so you can
  also just drag files onto the drive. Calibre earns its place for a tidy
  catalogue, bulk metadata and cover fixes, or format conversion for the rare
  file the device cannot open.
- **Comics and colour PDFs: send as-is.** Do not convert a CBZ, CBR or colour
  PDF to EPUB — you lose the layout and the colour images that make them worth
  reading on this screen.
- **Books land wherever you send them.** Calibre copies to the drive root or the
  "Books" folder; the device indexes them on disconnect.
- **Keep your originals as EPUB.** Convert only when needed, and keep the EPUB.
