Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order and copy books to a reader. The InkPad 4 works with it over USB out of the
box.

## What to know

- **Send EPUB — no conversion.** The InkPad 4 reads EPUB directly, so Calibre
  sends it as-is. There is no Kobo KEPUB step and no Kindle AZW3 step to worry
  about.
- **Calibre is optional here.** Because the device opens most formats itself, you
  can also just drag files onto the drive. Calibre earns its place if you want a
  tidy catalogue, bulk metadata and cover fixes, or format conversion for the
  rare file the InkPad 4 cannot open.
- **PDFs and comics: send as-is.** Do not let Calibre convert a PDF or a CBZ to
  EPUB for this screen — you lose the layout that makes them worth reading here.
- **Books land wherever you send them.** Calibre copies to the drive root or the
  "Books" folder; the device indexes them on disconnect.
- **Keep your originals as EPUB.** Convert only when needed, and keep the EPUB.
