steps:
  - instruction: Create a notebook and pick a template
    detail: >-
      From the library, create a new notebook. Choose a template — plain, lined,
      dotted, grid, planners, music staves and many more. Each page can have its
      own template, and you can change a page's template later from the page menu.
  - instruction: Learn the writing tools and layers
    detail: >-
      The toolbar has several pens (ballpoint, fineliner, marker, pencil,
      calligraphy, brush), a highlighter, an eraser, and a selection tool for
      moving, resizing and cutting handwriting. The Paper Pro supports layers, so
      you can keep, for example, a diagram and your annotations of it separate.
      Colour is available with the Paper Pro's screen.
  - instruction: Write on an imported PDF
    detail: >-
      Open a PDF and write directly on it. Your marks are stored as an annotation
      layer over the original, so the source page is never altered. You can also
      add pages to a PDF for extra working space.
  - instruction: Convert handwriting to text
    detail: >-
      In a notebook, open the page menu and choose "Convert to text", or select
      handwriting with the selection tool and convert just that. The result can
      be emailed or copied. Accuracy is good with clear printing and drops with
      fast cursive; it supports many languages, set per notebook.
  - instruction: Organise with folders and tags
    detail: >-
      The library uses folders, and you can add tags to notebooks and to
      individual pages, then filter by tag. On a device that fills up with
      notebooks quickly, tags are worth using from the start.
  - instruction: Export a notebook or annotated PDF
    detail: >-
      From a notebook or document menu, choose to email it, or export it as PDF
      or PNG. With the desktop and mobile apps you can also open your notes there.
      An annotated PDF exports with your marks merged or on a separate layer,
      depending on the export option.
  - instruction: Know what it does not do
    detail: >-
      There is no app store and no third-party apps in the normal software. You
      cannot import notes from another brand of tablet directly. Full-text search
      of handwriting is limited. And everything lives in reMarkable's system —
      you export to PDF or text, there is no open notebook file without developer
      mode.
---

The Paper Pro's note-taking is its whole reason to exist, and it is genuinely
good: a wide range of pens, layers, colour, per-page templates, and reliable
handwriting-to-text if you print rather than scrawl. The limits are that it is a
closed system best used through reMarkable's own apps, and that organisation is
down to you — use folders and tags early.
