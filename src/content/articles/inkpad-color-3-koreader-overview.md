PocketBook is one of the platforms KOReader supports directly. There is no
jailbreak and no exploit: you copy KOReader's application file into the device's
"applications" folder, and it shows up in the PocketBook app menu next to the
stock reader. Opening it starts KOReader; closing it returns you to PocketBook's
own software.

## Why add it

- **Comic and PDF handling** — per-document crop that is remembered, panel-fit
  modes, right-to-left support for manga, and a sharper reflow than the stock
  reader.
- **Colour-aware** — KOReader renders colour images on the Kaleido screen; you
  are not giving up colour by using it.
- **Finer typography** — margin, line-spacing and hyphenation control beyond
  what PocketBook exposes.
- **Consistency** — the same reading app, settings and synced position as your
  other devices.

## Why you might not

You lose the PocketBook store, Send-to-PocketBook delivery, Adobe-DRM library
books, text-to-speech and audiobook playback while inside KOReader. If you use
those, you will be switching between the two readers.
