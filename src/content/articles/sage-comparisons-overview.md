The Sage's case is a large Kobo with buttons, waterproofing and a stylus, in an
open ecosystem. It is from 2021, so it is the oldest of the current Kobo line.

- **Kobo Libra Colour.** The newer 7-inch Kobo. Smaller and lighter, colour
  screen, current-generation panel, also with buttons, waterproofing and stylus
  support. Unless you specifically want the Sage's bigger 8-inch page, the Libra
  Colour is the more up-to-date choice.
- **Kindle Scribe.** Amazon's 10.2-inch writing tablet. Much better at
  note-taking — proper notebooks, handwriting-to-text, PDF markup — but a closed
  Amazon device, not waterproof, and heavier. Choose the Scribe if writing is a
  major use; choose the Sage for an open reader that also takes notes.
- **Kobo Clara BW.** The small, cheap Kobo. No buttons, no waterproofing, no
  stylus, 6-inch screen. Choose it if you want the lightest plain reader and do
  not need the Sage's size or extras.
