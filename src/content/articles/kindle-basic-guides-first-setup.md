steps:
  - instruction: Charge the Kindle before setup
    detail: >-
      A new Kindle usually ships around half charged, and setup includes a
      software update. Plug it in with the supplied USB-C cable and give it time
      on the charger before you start.
  - instruction: Turn it on and choose your language and Wi-Fi network
    detail: >-
      Hold the power button on the bottom edge. Pick your language, then your
      Wi-Fi network and enter its password. The Kindle needs Wi-Fi once to
      register and to download its current firmware.
  - instruction: Sign in to your Amazon account
    detail: >-
      Enter the email and password for the Amazon account you want the Kindle
      tied to — this is "registering" the device. A registered Kindle can
      download your purchases and use Send to Kindle; an unregistered one can
      only read books you copy over by cable.
    tip: >-
      If the Kindle was a gift and is registered to someone else, go to Settings,
      then "Your Account", choose "Deregister", and register it to your own.
  - instruction: Let the software update install
    detail: >-
      The Kindle checks for and installs the latest firmware, shows a progress
      bar, and restarts once. Leave it plugged in until it finishes.
  - instruction: Understand the lock-screen ads
    detail: >-
      Most entry Kindles are sold "with Special Offers", which shows an
      advertisement on the sleep screen and a small banner on the home screen.
      They do not appear while you are reading. This is a permanent property of
      the device you paid a lower price for, not a setting you can switch off.
  - instruction: Remove the ads if you want to, from Amazon's website
    detail: >-
      On a computer, go to "Manage Your Content and Devices", open the Devices
      tab, select the Kindle, and look for "Special Offers" with an option to
      unsubscribe for a one-time fee (around 20 US dollars). After you pay and the
      Kindle syncs, the sleep screen switches to book covers or Amazon artwork.
  - instruction: Set the front light
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top of the screen for quick settings and adjust the
      brightness. The entry Kindle has a plain white front light only — there is
      no warm-light control on this model.
  - instruction: Add your books
    detail: >-
      For Amazon purchases, open the Library and tap a book to download it. For
      your own EPUB files, use Send to Kindle or connect the Kindle to a computer
      by USB and copy files into the "documents" folder.

Setting up the entry Kindle is quick. The only part that surprises people is the
ads: a Kindle sold "with Special Offers" will always have them unless you pay the
one-time fee through Amazon's website. Registration is what connects the device
to your account and purchases — an unregistered Kindle still works, but only for
books you load yourself over a cable.
