The entry Kindle is small, light and cheap, and it needs almost nothing. A case
that sleeps and wakes the device is the one purchase most people should make —
partly because it also means you see the lock-screen ad for less time. Because
it is the cheapest Kindle, it is worth keeping accessory spending modest; a
20-dollar case on a 110-dollar device is about the right ratio. Picks are grouped
by type below.
