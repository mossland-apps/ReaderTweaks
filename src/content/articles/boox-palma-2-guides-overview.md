The Palma 2 is a phone-shaped Android e-ink device — not a phone, but a pocket
reader you hold in one hand. This section covers setting up a new one, the apps
worth installing, using it one-handed, and the display settings that make
Android apps usable on e-ink.
[KOReader](/devices/boox/boox-palma-2/koreader/) and
[Calibre](/devices/boox/boox-palma-2/calibre/) have their own sections;
[Customization](/devices/boox/boox-palma-2/customization/) covers fonts, gestures
and the per-app optimisation dialog.
