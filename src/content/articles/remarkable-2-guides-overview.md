The reMarkable 2 is a writing tablet, so these guides are about documents and
notes rather than an ebook library. This section covers setting up a device
(new, used or refurbished), the several ways to get PDFs and EPUBs onto it, and
how the notebooks and handwriting conversion work.
[Calibre](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-2/calibre/) and
[KOReader](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-2/koreader/) have their own sections;
[Customization](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-2/customization/) covers templates
and writing settings.

## Start here

- **[Set up a reMarkable 2](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-2/guides/first-setup/)**
  — the account, the Connect subscription question, pairing the Marker, and what
  to check on a used unit.
- **[Get documents onto the reMarkable 2](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-2/guides/get-documents-on/)**
  — the desktop and mobile apps, email, cloud integrations, and why EPUB is
  awkward.
- **[Notebooks and handwriting conversion on the reMarkable 2](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-2/guides/notebooks-and-handwriting/)**
  — writing, converting to text, and exporting.
