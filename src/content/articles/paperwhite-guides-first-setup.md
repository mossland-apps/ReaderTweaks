steps:
  - instruction: Charge the Paperwhite before setup
    detail: >-
      A new Kindle usually ships around half charged, and setup includes a
      software update. Plug it in with the supplied USB-C cable and give it some
      time on the charger before you start.
  - instruction: Turn it on and choose your language and Wi-Fi network
    detail: >-
      Hold the power button on the bottom edge for a few seconds. Pick your
      language, then your Wi-Fi network and enter its password. The Kindle needs
      Wi-Fi once to register and to download its current firmware.
  - instruction: Sign in to your Amazon account
    detail: >-
      Enter the email and password for the Amazon account you want the Kindle
      tied to. This is called "registering" the device. A registered Kindle can
      download your purchases and use Send to Kindle; an unregistered one can
      only read books you copy over by USB cable.
    tip: >-
      If the Kindle was "bought for" you as a gift, it may already be registered
      to the giver's account. Go to Settings, then "Your Account", and choose
      "Deregister", then register it to your own.
  - instruction: Let the software update install
    detail: >-
      The Kindle checks for and installs the latest firmware, shows a progress
      bar, and restarts once. Leave it plugged in until it finishes and returns
      to the home screen.
  - instruction: Understand the lock-screen ads
    detail: >-
      Most Paperwhites are sold in a "with Special Offers" version, which shows
      an advertisement on the sleep screen and a small banner on the home
      screen. They do not appear while you are reading. This is a permanent
      property of the device that you paid a lower price for; it is not a setting
      you can switch off.
  - instruction: Remove the ads if you want to, from Amazon's website
    detail: >-
      On a computer, go to "Manage Your Content and Devices" in your Amazon
      account, open the Devices tab, select the Kindle, and look for "Special
      Offers" with an option to unsubscribe for a one-time fee (around 20 US
      dollars). After you pay and the Kindle syncs over Wi-Fi, the sleep screen
      switches to book covers or Amazon artwork.
  - instruction: Set the front light and warm light
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top of the screen for quick settings. The brightness
      slider controls the white light; a second control adds a warm amber tone,
      which most people raise in the evening. There is also an automatic
      schedule.
  - instruction: Add your books
    detail: >-
      For Amazon purchases, open the Library and tap a book to download it. For
      your own EPUB files, use Send to Kindle or connect the Kindle to a
      computer by USB and copy files into the "documents" folder.

Setting up a Paperwhite is quick. The only part that surprises people is the
ads: a Kindle sold "with Special Offers" will always have them unless you pay
the one-time fee to remove them through Amazon's website. Registration is what
connects the device to your account and your purchases — an unregistered Kindle
still works, but only for books you load yourself over a cable.
