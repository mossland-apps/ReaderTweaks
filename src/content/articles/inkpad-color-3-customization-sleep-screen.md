  - instruction: Decide between a book cover and your own images
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Personalize", the InkPad Color 3 can show the cover
      of the book you are reading — in colour — on the sleep screen. This guide
      is for the other option: showing your own fixed images.
  - instruction: Prepare your images
    detail: >-
      The screen is 1404 by 1872 pixels. Portrait images at that size look best.
      Colour images work, but remember the Kaleido screen shows them muted — a

  - instruction: Connect the device and add the images
    detail: >-

  - instruction: Test it
    detail: >-
      Press the power button once to sleep the device and confirm your image
      appears. Power off completely to check the power-off screen.
  - instruction: To go back to the default
    detail: >-
      In the same Personalize menu, set the Logo and Power-off Logo back to
      "Standard" or "Book cover". The built-in artwork returns.

PocketBook makes custom sleep screens a normal setting — no developer menu.
Copy an image into the "system/logo" folder, then choose it under Personalize.
The InkPad Color 3 shows the sleep image in colour, so a favourite cover or
photo works well; just expect the muted Kaleido rendering rather than
screen-accurate colour.
