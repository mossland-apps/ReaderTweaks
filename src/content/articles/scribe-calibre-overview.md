Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order and copy books onto a reader. With the Scribe it works over USB out of the
box, and can also send books to your Kindle email address.

## What to know

- **Send AZW3, not EPUB.** The Scribe does not read EPUB copied over by cable.
  Calibre converts to AZW3 automatically when it sends to a Kindle and keeps your
  EPUB copy.
- **Send PDFs unchanged.** Do not convert PDFs for the Scribe. Its 10.2-inch
  screen shows a full-size page well, and converting a PDF to a Kindle format
  usually makes it worse. Calibre copies PDFs across as-is.
- **KFX is optional.** The community "KFX Output" plugin gives slightly better
  typography for reflowable books. AZW3 is fine for most reading; add KFX only if
  you want its features.
- **Notebooks are not in Calibre's reach.** Calibre manages books you send to
  the Scribe. The handwritten notebooks live in Amazon's system and are exported
  by email, not through Calibre.
