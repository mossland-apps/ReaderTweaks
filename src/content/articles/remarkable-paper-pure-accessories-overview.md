The Paper Pure is large and comes with its Marker, so the accessories that
matter are protecting it and keeping it writing. A folio is the first purchase.
Replacement Marker tips are a consumable you will need regularly; the Paper Pure
uses the current-generation tips, the same as the Paper Pro, not the reMarkable
2's. Because there is no front light, a clip-on reading light is genuinely useful
here for anyone who writes or reads in bed or a dim room. Picks are grouped by
type below, with a note on which are cut for this device and which fit any
reader.
