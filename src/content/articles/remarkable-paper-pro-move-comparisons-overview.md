The Paper Pro Move is a reMarkable shrunk to pocket size: the same writing
experience, colour screen and app on a 7.3-inch body light enough to hold one-
handed. It is still a poor ebook reader, and the small page limits how much of a
document or notebook you see at once.

- **reMarkable Paper Pro.** The full-size 11.8-inch version. Same software and
  Marker, far more room for PDFs, diagrams and side-by-side notes, but heavier
  and not portable in the same way. Choose the Paper Pro if writing at a desk is
  the main use; choose the Move if you want to carry it everywhere and accept a
  smaller working area.
- **BOOX Go Color 7.** A 7-inch Android tablet that runs any app — good note
  apps, and every reading app — so it is a much better ebook reader and far more
  flexible. Its note-taking is app-dependent rather than a designed whole, and
  battery life is shorter. Choose the BOOX for flexibility and reading; choose
  the Move for a focused, distraction-free writing device.
- **Kobo Libra Colour.** A proper 7-inch e-reader with light stylus notes,
  waterproofing and an open ecosystem, at a lower price. If your writing needs
  are light and reading matters more, it does both jobs for less; the Move is
  the better pure writing tool.
