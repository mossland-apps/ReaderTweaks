steps:
  - instruction: Charge the Page before setup
    detail: >-
      A new device often ships part-charged, and setup includes a system update.
      Plug it in with the supplied USB-C cable and give it time on the charger
      before you start.
  - instruction: Turn it on and connect to Wi-Fi
    detail: >-
      Press the power button on the top edge. Choose your language and time zone,
      then your Wi-Fi network and enter the password. The device needs Wi-Fi to
      update and to reach the Play Store.
  - instruction: Install any firmware update
    detail: >-
      Open Settings, then "Firmware Update", and apply anything offered. BOOX
      updates regularly and the updates improve how Android apps render on e-ink,
      so it is worth doing now.
  - instruction: Decide about a BOOX account
    detail: >-
      A BOOX (Onyx) account is used for the BOOX cloud — syncing notes, reading
      position in the stock NeoReader app, and configuration backups across BOOX
      devices. It is optional. If you plan to read mainly in the Kindle, Kobo or
      KOReader apps, you can skip it.
  - instruction: Turn on the Google Play Store
    detail: >-
      Open the Apps screen and find "Play Store". On first launch, BOOX shows a
      screen to "enable Google Play"; tap through it, wait a minute or two while
      it registers the device with Google, then restart the device if prompted.
      Once done, sign in with your Google account.
    tip: >-
      If the Play Store says "device not certified" or apps will not install,
      restart the device, wait a few hours, and try again — Google's registration
      can take time to propagate. It almost always clears on its own.
  - instruction: Set up the fingerprint reader
    detail: >-
      The fingerprint sensor is built into the power button. Under Settings, then
      "Fingerprint" (or "Security"), add one or two fingers. It then unlocks the
      device with a press, which is quicker than a PIN on e-ink.
  - instruction: Learn the control centre and the buttons
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top-right for the BOOX control centre, which has the
      refresh-mode buttons, front-light sliders (brightness and warm tone), and a
      full-screen refresh button that clears ghosting. The two page-turn buttons
      on the wide grip turn pages in most reading apps and can be remapped later.
  - instruction: Set a comfortable default refresh mode
    detail: >-
      In the control centre, leave the global refresh mode on "HD" (or "Regal")
      for reading. The faster modes are for scrolling and menus and make text
      look grey. Per-app settings override this, covered in a later guide.

Setting up the Page is a normal Android first-run with one extra step: enabling
the Google Play Store, which BOOX ships switched off. Once that is done and you
have signed in to Google, the device behaves like any Android reader that
happens to have an e-ink screen, page-turn buttons and a fingerprint sensor. The
BOOX account is optional and only matters if you use the stock apps or own more
than one BOOX.
