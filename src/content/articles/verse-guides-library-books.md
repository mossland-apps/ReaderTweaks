steps:
  - instruction: Understand why the Verse is good at this
    detail: >-
      Most public-library ebooks are EPUB or PDF files locked with Adobe DRM. A
      Kindle cannot open them at all; a Kobo can, through its own store
      integration. The Verse opens them the direct way — it has an Adobe DRM
      reader built in, so once it is authorised you can side-load a loan and read
      it with no computer in the loop.
  - instruction: Authorise the Verse with an Adobe ID
    detail: >-
      Create a free Adobe account at adobe.com if you do not have one. On the
      Verse, open Settings, then "Accounts and Synchronization", then "Adobe
      DRM", and sign in. This is a one-time step. Use the same Adobe ID
      everywhere you read loans so books unlock on every device.
  - instruction: Borrow the book
    detail: >-
      In the Libby app or on your library's OverDrive website, borrow the title.
      When it asks how to read it, choose the option for "another app" or
      "download EPUB" / "Adobe EPUB" rather than "Kindle" or "read in browser".
  - instruction: Get the ".acsm" file
    detail: >-
      Downloading an Adobe loan gives you a small file ending in ".acsm". This is
      not the book — it is a ticket the reader exchanges for the book. Save it
      somewhere you can reach it: your computer's Downloads folder, or your
      phone.
  - instruction: Copy the ".acsm" to the Verse and open it there
    detail: >-
      Connect the Verse by USB (or email the ".acsm" to your Send-to-PocketBook
      address) and copy the ".acsm" file into any folder. On the device, open it
      from the Library. With Adobe DRM authorised, the Verse contacts Adobe,
      downloads the real EPUB or PDF, and replaces the ".acsm" entry with the
      book.
    warning: >-
      Open the ".acsm" on the Verse itself. If you open it first in a computer
      program such as Adobe Digital Editions, the book is fulfilled to that
      computer and copying the resulting EPUB to the Verse usually will not work.
  - instruction: Return a loan early (optional)
    detail: >-
      Loans expire on their own and simply stop opening. To return one early and
      free up a hold slot, use the Libby app or the OverDrive website — the Verse
      itself does not have a "return" button for side-loaded loans.

The Verse is one of the few readers that opens Adobe-DRM library books without a
computer. The routine is: authorise an Adobe ID on the device once, borrow the
title as an Adobe EPUB, then move the small ".acsm" file to the Verse and open
it there to pull down the book. If a loan will not open, it is nearly always an
Adobe ID mismatch — the troubleshooting page walks through that.
