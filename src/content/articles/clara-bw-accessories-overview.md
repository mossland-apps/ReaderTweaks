The Clara BW is a plain reader and needs very little. A case is the one purchase
most people should make; it protects a device that is small enough to disappear
into a bag, and a folio with a sleep magnet saves battery. If you listen to Kobo
audiobooks, a set of Bluetooth earbuds is the other thing worth having, since
the Clara BW has no speaker or headphone socket. Picks are grouped by type
below, with a note on which are cut for this device and which fit any reader.
