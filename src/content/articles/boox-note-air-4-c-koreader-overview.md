The Note Air 4 C runs Android, so KOReader is just an app. You install it from
the Google Play Store or by sideloading the Android APK — no jailbreak, no
launcher to register, and the stock BOOX software is untouched.

## Why put it on a Note Air 4 C

The BOOX stock reader (NeoReader) is capable and handles PDFs and pen markup
well, so the reasons to add KOReader are specific:

- **Typography and layout control** beyond what NeoReader or the store apps
  expose — fine margin, hyphenation and font-weight settings, on a large page.
- **Format range** — CBZ, CBR, FB2, DjVu and a genuinely usable PDF reflow, all
  in one app.
- **Its own refresh handling** — KOReader manages e-ink refresh itself and often
  looks cleaner than a generic app forced through BOOX's optimisation.
- **Consistency** — the same reading app, settings and synced position as your
  other devices.

## What to know

- KOReader will not open DRM-protected store books (Kindle, Kobo purchases,
  library loans). Use the store apps for those.
- KOReader has no pen or handwriting support — keep NeoReader or BOOX Notes for
  markup.
- After installing, set KOReader's BOOX per-app refresh mode to HD or Regal,
  black-and-white, and let KOReader control full refresh from inside its own
  settings.
