The Libra Colour's case for existing is the combination of physical page-turn
buttons, a colour screen and waterproofing in a 7-inch body. The devices below
each drop one or more of those.

- **Kobo Clara BW.** Smaller, cheaper, black-and-white, no buttons, not
  waterproof. The right choice if you want the lightest possible plain reader
  and do not care about colour or a physical page turn.
- **Kobo Sage.** Larger 8-inch screen, also has buttons and a stylus, but it is
  black-and-white and from an older generation. Consider it if screen size
  matters more than colour.
- **Kindle Paperwhite.** Same 7-inch size and also waterproof, but
  black-and-white, no buttons, and tied to Amazon's ecosystem, which makes
  sideloading and KOReader harder. Choose it only if you are committed to Kindle
  and Amazon purchases.
