The Verse is the budget PocketBook. It keeps the brand's strengths — buttons you
can remap, a warm front light, a microSD slot, open format support and on-device
Adobe-DRM library books — and drops audio, Bluetooth, waterproofing and the
sharper 300-ppi screen.

- **PocketBook Verse Pro.** The same body and software with a 300-ppi screen,
  IPX8 waterproofing, Bluetooth, text-to-speech and audiobook playback. If you
  want any of those, pay the difference for the Pro; if you only read text and
  keep the device at home, the Verse does the same core job for less.
- **Kobo Clara BW.** The same 6-inch size and price bracket, but no buttons, no
  microSD, no waterproofing, and a closed format list — though Kobo's store and
  software are more polished, and library books work through the built-in
  OverDrive integration rather than a manual Adobe step. Choose the Clara BW for
  the smoother store; choose the Verse for buttons, a card slot and format
  freedom.
- **Basic Kindle.** Amazon's cheapest reader, tied to the Kindle store, with no
  buttons and no support for library EPUBs at all. Choose it only if you buy
  from Amazon and want the simplest possible device.
