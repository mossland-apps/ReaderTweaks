steps:
  - instruction: Understand where documents live
    detail: >-
      Everything on the Paper Pure syncs through your reMarkable account. You add
      a document in one place — the desktop app, the mobile app, email or a
      linked cloud folder — and it appears on the device after a sync over Wi-Fi.
      There is no plain USB drive to drag files onto.
  - instruction: Method A — the desktop app
    detail: >-
      Open the reMarkable app on your computer, sign in, and drag PDF or EPUB
      files into the library, or use "Import". They upload to your account and
      download to the Paper Pure on its next sync. This is the main method for a
      batch of PDFs.
  - instruction: Method B — the mobile app and share sheet
    detail: >-
      On your phone, open a PDF and use "Share", then the reMarkable app, or add
      it inside the app. Good for a document that arrived by email on your phone.
  - instruction: Method C — email to your device
    detail: >-
      With a reMarkable account you can email documents to a personal address
      shown in the account settings. Attachments are added to your library.
      Availability of this depends on your plan.
  - instruction: Method D — cloud integrations
    detail: >-
      With a reMarkable Connect subscription you can link Google Drive, Dropbox or
      OneNote and open files from them directly on the device. Without the
      subscription these integrations are limited or unavailable.
  - instruction: Know the EPUB limitation
    detail: >-
      The Paper Pure opens EPUB, but its handling is basic — limited font and
      layout control, no library organisation to speak of, and some EPUBs render
      poorly. For serious ebook reading, a Kobo or Kindle is far better. Use the
      Paper Pure for PDFs and for the occasional EPUB you also want to mark up.
  - instruction: Convert an EPUB to PDF if it renders badly
    detail: >-
      If an EPUB looks wrong on the device, convert it to PDF in Calibre first,
      choosing a page size close to the Paper Pure's screen. You lose reflow but
      gain a layout that displays and annotates reliably.
---

The Paper Pure has no USB file mode — documents come in through the reMarkable
apps, email, or cloud links, and sync to the device over Wi-Fi. The desktop app
is the workhorse for PDFs. Treat EPUB as a secondary format here: it opens, but
the Paper Pure is not built to be your ebook reader, and a Kobo or Kindle does
that job much better.
