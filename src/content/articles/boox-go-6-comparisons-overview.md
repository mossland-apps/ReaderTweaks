The Go 6 is the smallest, lightest, plainest BOOX: a 6-inch black-and-white
screen, no buttons, no speaker, but full Android with the Play Store and a
microSD slot.

- **BOOX Go Color 7.** The bigger sibling — 7-inch, colour, with physical
  page-turn buttons and a speaker. If you want colour, buttons, or a larger
  page, pick the Go Color 7; the Go 6 is the choice for the lightest possible
  Android reader.
- **Kobo Clara BW.** The same 6-inch black-and-white size, but a dedicated Kobo
  rather than Android. It works out of the box, has much better standby, and its
  store and software are more polished — but it cannot run the Kindle app or
  arbitrary apps. Choose the Clara BW for a simple long-lasting reader; choose
  the Go 6 to run every store's app on one device.
- **Kindle Paperwhite.** A locked 7-inch Amazon reader, waterproof, with the best
  standby of the four. Choose it only if you buy from the Kindle store. The Go 6
  can run the Kindle app and everything else.
