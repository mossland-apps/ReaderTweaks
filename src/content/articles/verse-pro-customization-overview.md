PocketBook's software is unusually configurable. Everything in this section is a
normal setting or a file you copy on — there is no developer menu to unlock and
no jailbreak.

## What you can change

- **Fonts.** Add your own TrueType or OpenType fonts by copying them to a
  "fonts" folder or through the reading menu.
- **The buttons.** Assign a short press and a long press of each page-turn
  button to actions you choose — next page, bookmark, dictionary, screen refresh
  and more.
- **The sleep and power-off screens.** Point the device at your own images in
  Settings, with no hidden menu.
- **Reading layout.** Fonts, spacing, margins, hyphenation and PDF reflow, all
  in the reading menu.
- **The status bar and gestures.** Choose what the top and bottom bars show and
  what edge swipes do.
