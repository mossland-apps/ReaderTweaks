The reMarkable 2 is large and, if bought used, may not come with a case, so a
folio is usually the first purchase. Replacement Marker tips are a consumable —
make sure the pack is for the reMarkable 2's Marker, not the newer Paper Pro
generation. Because there is no front light, a clip-on reading light is
genuinely useful for anyone who writes in bed or a dim room. The reMarkable 2
has been out for years, so third-party folios, tips and protectors for it are
plentiful and cheap. Picks are grouped by type below, with a note on which are
cut for this device and which fit any reader.
