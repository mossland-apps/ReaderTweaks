steps:
  - instruction: Check whether your Kindle even has ads
    detail: >-
      On the Kindle, open Settings and look for a "Special Offers" entry. If it
      is there, your device is the ad-supported version. If there is no such
      entry, you have the ad-free version and there is nothing to do — your
      sleep screen already shows book covers or Amazon artwork.
  - instruction: Understand what the ads are
    detail: >-
      A "with Special Offers" Kindle shows a full-screen advertisement while it
      sleeps and a small banner strip at the bottom of the home screen. Ads
      never appear while you are reading a book. Amazon sold the device at a
      lower price in exchange for this.
  - instruction: Remove the ads by paying the one-time fee
    detail: >-
      On a computer, go to "Manage Your Content and Devices" in your Amazon
      account and open the Devices tab. Select your Paperwhite. Find the
      "Special Offers" line and choose the option to unsubscribe. Amazon charges
      a one-time fee, usually around 20 US dollars, and the change is permanent
      for that device.
    tip: >-
      It is sometimes worth contacting Amazon customer service and simply asking
      to have Special Offers removed. They occasionally waive the fee, especially
      for a recently bought device.
  - instruction: Sync the Kindle so the change takes effect
    detail: >-
      Turn Wi-Fi on and, from the quick settings or Settings menu, choose "Sync"
      or restart the device. The sleep screen switches to covers or artwork
      within a minute or two of syncing.
  - instruction: If you are not going to pay — what you can still do
    detail: >-
      You cannot replace the ads with your own images or turn them off in
      settings. What you can do is make them less intrusive: keep the Kindle in a
      case that sleeps and wakes it so you see the ad for less time, and turn
      off "Special Offers notifications" under Settings, then Notifications, so
      the banner is quieter. The home-screen banner cannot be fully removed on an
      ad-supported device.
---

The lock-screen ads on a Paperwhite are a property of the hardware you bought,
not a setting. The only real fix is the one-time payment through Amazon's
website, which converts the device to the ad-free version for good. Everything
else is just reducing how often you see them.
