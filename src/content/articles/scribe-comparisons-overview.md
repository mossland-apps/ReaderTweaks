The Scribe is a large writing tablet that is also a full Kindle. That
combination is its whole case: if you want Amazon's store and library on the
same device you take notes on, nothing else does both as well.

- **reMarkable Paper Pro.** The Scribe's direct rival as a writing device, and
  the better one for writing alone — a bigger 11.8-inch screen, a front light,
  colour, more paper-like feel, deeper note organisation, and open export
  (PDF, PNG, SVG) with third-party sync. But it is a weak ebook reader: no
  Kindle or Kobo store, awkward EPUB handling, and a subscription for full cloud
  features. Choose the reMarkable if writing is the point and reading is
  secondary; choose the Scribe if you want one device for Kindle books and
  notes.
- **Kindle Paperwhite.** The question of whether you need a large device at all.
  The Paperwhite is a 7-inch waterproof reader with no pen, at a fraction of the
  price and weight. If you do not actually write or read many PDFs, it is the
  better everyday Kindle. Choose the Scribe only if the pen and the big screen
  earn their place.
- **Kobo Sage.** Kobo's writing-capable reader — 8-inch, has page-turn buttons,
  waterproof, takes a stylus, and runs in an open ecosystem where sideloading
  and KOReader are easy. Its note-taking is more basic than the Scribe's. Choose
  the Sage if you want light note-taking plus an open reader; choose the Scribe
  for serious note-taking inside Amazon's world.
