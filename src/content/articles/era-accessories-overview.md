The Era is well-built and needs little. A folio case that sleeps and wakes the
device on open and close is the one purchase most people should make; look for
one that leaves the raised side grip and its buttons uncovered. The Era has a
built-in speaker, so headphones are optional — but a set of Bluetooth earbuds is
worth having for private listening on a train or next to someone. There is no
microSD slot, so a card is not an option here. Picks are grouped by type below,
with a note on which are cut for this device and which fit any reader.
