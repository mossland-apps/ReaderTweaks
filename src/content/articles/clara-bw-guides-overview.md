gestures that stand in for the physical buttons it does not have, and its
Bluetooth audio for audiobooks. The
[KOReader](/devices/kobo/clara-bw/koreader/) and
[Calibre](/devices/kobo/clara-bw/calibre/) sections cover those programs;
[Customization](/devices/kobo/clara-bw/customization/) covers fonts and screens.
