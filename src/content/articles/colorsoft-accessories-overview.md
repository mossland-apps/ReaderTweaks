The Colorsoft needs almost nothing. A case that sleeps and wakes the device is
the one purchase most people should make. Two of Amazon's newer accessories, the
Page-Turn Cover and the charging dock, work only with the **Signature Edition**,
so check which model you have before buying them. Earlier Colorsoft models and
the 2026 model use different case sizes, so buy a case listed for your exact
model. Picks are grouped by type below.
