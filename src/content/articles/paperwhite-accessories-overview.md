The Paperwhite needs almost nothing. A case that sleeps and wakes the device is
the one purchase most people should make, partly because it also means you see
the lock-screen ad for less time. The rest depends on how and where you read.
Picks are grouped by type below, with a note on which are cut for this device
and which fit any reader.
