steps:
  - instruction: Decide which apps you actually need
    detail: >-
      The Page can run all of them, but each app you sign into is another account
      and another thing syncing. Common picks are the Kindle app for Amazon
      purchases, the Kobo app for Kobo store and library books, Libby for public
      library loans, and a general reader such as Moon+ Reader or ReadEra for
      your own files. KOReader has its own section.
  - instruction: Install from the Play Store
    detail: >-
      Open the Play Store, search for the app, and install it as you would on a
      phone. If an app is missing from search, try the Play Store website in the
      device's browser, or sideload the APK from a trusted source such as the
      developer's site.
  - instruction: Open the app once, then open BOOX's per-app settings
    detail: >-
      Launch the app so Android registers it. Then go to the Apps screen, find
      the app, and open its BOOX "optimisation" panel — either a long-press
      option, or Settings, then "App optimisation". This dialog is where you fix
      how the app looks and behaves.
  - instruction: Set the display and refresh options per app
    detail: >-
      In the optimisation panel, for a text reading app choose a clean refresh
      mode (Regal or Normal), turn on "bleach" or "enhance contrast" only if text
      looks grey, and enable "full refresh on page turn" if you see heavy
      ghosting. Getting this right per app is the single biggest quality
      difference on a BOOX.
  - instruction: Control background behaviour
    detail: >-
      In the same area, or under Settings, then "Power", set reading apps you
      only open occasionally to be "optimised" or allowed to be closed in the
      background, so they do not drain the battery syncing. Keep an app you use
      constantly exempt so it resumes instantly.
  - instruction: Turn off in-app animations and effects
    detail: >-
      In each app's own settings, turn off page-turn animations, "curl" effects
      and auto-brightness — they look bad and stutter on e-ink. Set the app's own
      theme to a plain light mode.
  - instruction: Check the page-turn buttons work in your reader
    detail: >-
      Most reading apps respond to the Page's physical buttons for next and
      previous page. If one does not, check that app's own controls settings, or
      use BOOX's button-mapping under Settings — covered in the customization
      section.
---

The Page's strength is that it runs the real Kindle, Kobo, Libby and other apps,
so you are not locked to one store, and it has physical buttons to turn their
pages. The price is that each app needs two rounds of setup: the app's own
account and settings, and BOOX's per-app optimisation panel that controls
refresh and background behaviour. Spend the ten minutes per app — it is the
difference between a Page that feels good and one that feels broken.
