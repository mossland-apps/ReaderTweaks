Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order and copy books onto a reader. With the entry Kindle it works over USB out
of the box, and can also send books to your Kindle email address.

## What to know

- **Send AZW3, not EPUB.** The Kindle does not read EPUB files copied over by
  cable. Calibre converts to AZW3 (also called KF8) automatically when it sends
  to a Kindle and keeps your EPUB copy.
- **KFX is Amazon's newer format** with slightly better typography. Calibre can
  produce it with the community "KFX Output" plugin. AZW3 is fine for most
  reading; add KFX only if you notice the difference.
- **Books land in "Documents"** and sync their furthest page read across your
  devices if "Whispersync for personal documents" is on.
- **Watch the storage.** 16 GB is plenty for text but fills faster with PDFs.
- **Keep your originals as EPUB.**
