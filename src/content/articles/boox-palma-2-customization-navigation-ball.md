steps:
  - instruction: Turn on volume-key page turns
    detail: >-
      In BOOX Settings, open "System", then "Volume control", and set the volume
      keys to turn pages. This works in NeoReader and most third-party readers,
      and it is the single most useful change on a device with no buttons.
  - instruction: Enable the Navigation Ball
    detail: >-
      In Settings, open "Navigation Ball" (sometimes under "System" or
      "Function"). Turn it on. A small floating dot appears; tap it and it fans
      out into a ring of shortcuts.
  - instruction: Assign the Navigation Ball actions
    detail: >-
      Set the slots to the things you reach for most — full refresh, back, home,
      screenshot, and a brightness or refresh-mode toggle. Keep it to four or
      five so the ring stays quick to read.
  - instruction: Position the ball for your thumb
    detail: >-
      Drag the dot to a bottom corner where your thumb rests when you hold the
      Palma 2 one-handed. It stays put across apps.
  - instruction: Check the in-app gestures
    detail: >-
      In NeoReader's settings, review the tap zones and swipe actions. On a
      one-handed device, set the "previous page" zone somewhere your thumb can
      reach without shuffling your grip — many people move it to the bottom.
  - instruction: Optional — a third-party gesture app
    detail: >-
      If you want more, an e-ink-friendly launcher or gesture app from the Play
      Store can add edge swipes, but the built-in tools cover most needs.
---

The Palma 2 has no physical page-turn buttons, so the volume keys, the
Navigation Ball and the in-app tap zones do that job. Turn on volume-key page
turns first, then put the Navigation Ball where your thumb naturally sits and
fill it with the actions you use most.
