The Page is an Android device, so most of the work is choosing and tuning apps
rather than fighting a locked ecosystem. This section covers setting up a new
one, installing the reading apps you want, and the display settings that make
Android apps usable on an e-ink screen.
[KOReader](/devices/boox/boox-page/koreader/) and
[Calibre](/devices/boox/boox-page/calibre/) have their own sections;
[Customization](/devices/boox/boox-page/customization/) covers fonts, the
buttons and gestures, and the per-app optimisation dialog.

## Start here

- **[Set up a new BOOX Page](/devices/boox/boox-page/guides/first-setup/)** — the
  BOOX account, Google sign-in, the Play Store, and the fingerprint reader.
- **[Install reading apps on the BOOX Page](/devices/boox/boox-page/guides/install-reading-apps/)**
  — Kindle, Kobo, Libby and others, and what to do right after installing each.
- **[Make Android apps look right on the BOOX Page](/devices/boox/boox-page/guides/refresh-modes/)**
  — the refresh modes and why the stock settings look wrong.
