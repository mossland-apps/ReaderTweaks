The Paper Pure is a writing tablet, so these guides are about documents and
notes rather than an ebook library. This section covers setting up a new one,
the several ways to get PDFs and EPUBs onto it, and how the notebooks and
handwriting conversion work.
[Calibre](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pure/calibre/) and
[KOReader](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pure/koreader/) have their own
sections;
[Customization](/devices/remarkable/remarkable-paper-pure/customization/) covers
templates and writing settings.
