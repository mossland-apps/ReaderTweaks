steps:
  - instruction: Set your writing hand and toolbar side
    detail: >-
      In Settings, then "General" (or "Stylus" / "Handedness"), choose left or
      right. This moves the floating toolbar to the other edge and tunes palm
      rejection. On a small screen the toolbar takes up a bigger share of the
      page, so having it on the correct side matters more here.
  - instruction: Adjust palm rejection if you still get stray marks
    detail: >-
      If the side of your hand still draws, confirm handedness is correct, and
      start each stroke with the Marker tip on the screen before you rest your
      palm.
  - instruction: Turn page-turn taps on or off
    detail: >-
      Edge taps to turn pages are handy one-handed, but on a narrow screen it is
      easy to trigger them while writing near the edge. Turn them off if that
      happens; swiping still works.
  - instruction: Set a default pen and thickness
    detail: >-
      Pick the pen you use most and set its default thickness and colour. On the
      Move's colour screen it is worth setting a default ink colour you like, so
      new notebooks open ready to write.
  - instruction: Assign the Marker Plus shortcut button
    detail: >-
      If you have the Marker Plus, its barrel button has a shortcut. In Settings,
      then "Stylus", set it to "eraser" (hold to erase, release to return to the
      pen) or to cycle the last two tools. The eraser option is the popular
      choice, and quicker than reaching a small toolbar.
  - instruction: Set the front light for the room
    detail: >-
      Swipe down for the light control. The Move has a front light — run it low
      in a dim room and off in daylight to save its smaller battery. This is the
      setting people adjust most on a device they use in changing light.
  - instruction: Control how PDFs fit the screen
    detail: >-
      Open a PDF, open its view menu, and set the fit — "fit width", "fit page",
      or a custom zoom. A full-size page is small on the Move; zoom in and pan,
      or prepare a margin-trimmed or resized copy in Calibre so it fills the page
      without constant zooming.
---

None of these are hidden or risky — they are the normal settings that decide
whether the Move feels right in one hand. Set handedness and toolbar side first,
then a default pen and ink colour, then the Marker button, then get comfortable
with the front light. Spend a minute per PDF on the fit and zoom, or prepare
documents in Calibre so you do not have to.
