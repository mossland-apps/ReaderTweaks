PocketBook's software is more open than Kobo's or Amazon's: you can copy almost
any book file straight onto the device and it will open. This section covers
setting up a new Verse Pro, the several ways to load books, and its
text-to-speech and audio playback. The
[Calibre](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-verse-pro/calibre/) and
[KOReader](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-verse-pro/koreader/) sections cover
those; [Customization](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-verse-pro/customization/)
covers fonts, remapping the buttons and the sleep screen.

## Start here

- **[Set up a new PocketBook Verse Pro](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-verse-pro/guides/first-setup/)**
  — accounts, Wi-Fi, and the Adobe ID for library books.
- **[Get books onto the PocketBook Verse Pro](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-verse-pro/guides/get-books-on/)**
  — USB, the Send-to-PocketBook email service, and cloud sync.
- **[Read aloud and play audiobooks on the PocketBook Verse Pro](/devices/pocketbook/pocketbook-verse-pro/guides/audio-and-tts/)**
  — text-to-speech, your own audio files, and Bluetooth.
