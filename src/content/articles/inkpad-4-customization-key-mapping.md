steps:
  - instruction: Open the key-mapping settings
    detail: >-
      From the home screen, open Settings, then "Personalize", then "Key mapping"
      (on some firmware it is under "Reading settings"). You will see a list of
      the physical controls the InkPad 4 has.
  - instruction: Understand short press and long press
    detail: >-
      Each of the two bottom page-turn buttons has two actions you can set
      separately — a short press and a long press (holding the button for about a
      second). That gives you four assignable actions from two buttons.
  - instruction: Set the short presses for turning pages
    detail: >-
      Most people leave short press on the right button as "Next page" and the
      left as "Previous page". Swap them if you prefer, or if you usually hold
      the device in one particular hand.
  - instruction: Assign the long presses to something useful
    detail: >-
      Long press is where the value is. Options usually include "Add bookmark",
      "Dictionary", "Screen refresh" (a full flash to clear ghosting),
      "Brightness", "Rotate" and "Table of contents". On a large screen used for
      PDFs, "Rotate" and "Screen refresh" are popular long-press choices.
  - instruction: Note how the buttons behave in landscape
    detail: >-
      When you rotate the InkPad 4, PocketBook keeps "next" and "previous" doing
      the sensible thing for the new orientation. If it feels backwards after a
      rotate, it is usually the PDF's own page order, not the button mapping.
  - instruction: Save and test
    detail: >-
      The changes take effect immediately. Open a book and try each press and
      hold to confirm they do what you expect. KOReader keeps its own separate
      mapping if you run it.
---

Two bottom buttons with a short and long press each give you four actions to
assign. The short presses are usually best left as page turns (or reversed),
while on a large-screen reader the long presses are worth spending on a manual
screen refresh and a rotate toggle for wide PDFs.
