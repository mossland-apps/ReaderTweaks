The Scribe is large and comes with its pen, so the accessories that matter are
about protecting it and keeping it writing. A folio cover that holds the pen is
the first purchase. Replacement pen tips are a consumable you will need
eventually. A paper-feel screen protector is worth it only if you do a lot of
writing and want more friction. Picks are grouped by type below, with a note on
which are cut for this device and which fit any reader.
