steps:
  - instruction: Charge the Colorsoft before setup
    detail: >-
      A new Kindle usually ships part charged, and setup includes a software
      update. Plug it in with the USB-C cable. Amazon quotes a full charge in
      under 2.5 hours with a 20 W USB-C adapter; a lower-power adapter or a
      computer port takes longer.
  - instruction: Turn it on and choose your language and Wi-Fi network
    detail: >-
      Hold the power button on the bottom edge for a few seconds. Pick your
      language, then your Wi-Fi network, and enter its password. The Colorsoft
      can use both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks.
  - instruction: Sign in to your Amazon account
    detail: >-
      Enter the email and password for the Amazon account you want the Kindle
      tied to. This is called "registering" the device. A registered Kindle can
      download your purchases and use Send to Kindle; an unregistered one can
      only read books you copy over by USB cable.
    tip: >-
      If the Kindle was "bought for" you as a gift, it may already be registered
      to the giver's account. Go to Settings, then "Your Account", and choose
      "Deregister", then register it to your own.
  - instruction: Let the software update install
    detail: >-
      The Kindle checks for and installs the latest firmware, shows a progress
      bar, and restarts. Leave it plugged in until it returns to the home
      screen.
    warning: >-
      If you are thinking of running KOReader, read the KOReader section first.
      Letting a Kindle update to the newest firmware can rule out the current
      jailbreak.
  - instruction: Check whether your model shows lock-screen ads
    detail: >-
      Amazon lists the 2026 Colorsoft as having no lock-screen ads. Some earlier
      units were sold "with Special Offers". Open Settings and look for a
      "Special Offers" entry — if there is none, your Kindle is ad-free and there
      is nothing to do.
  - instruction: Set the front light and warm light
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top of the screen for quick settings. The brightness
      slider controls the light; a second control adds a warm amber tone, which
      many people raise in the evening. There is also an automatic schedule.
    tip: >-
      Colour looks noticeably richer with the light turned up a little, even in
      daylight. If colours look dull, raise the brightness before deciding the
      screen is at fault.
  - instruction: Add your books
    detail: >-
      For Amazon purchases, open the Library and tap a book to download it. For
      your own files, use Send to Kindle or connect the Kindle to a computer by
      USB and copy files into the "documents" folder.

Setting up a Colorsoft is the same as any Kindle: charge it, join Wi-Fi,
register it to your Amazon account and let it update. The two things worth
doing straight away are setting the light — colour looks better with it on —
and checking whether your unit has lock-screen ads, since that depends on which
model you bought.
