Setting up a Verse Pro is quick. Two optional accounts are worth understanding:
a PocketBook account unlocks the wireless delivery and cloud features, and an
Adobe ID lets the device open DRM-protected library books directly. Neither is
needed just to read files you copy on by cable. The buttons and the SMARTlight
are the two hardware features to get familiar with early.
