Calibre is the free desktop program most people use to keep an ebook library in
order. Getting books from Calibre to the Page works a few ways, because it is an
Android device rather than a dedicated reader Calibre has a driver for.

## What to know

- **Send EPUB — no conversion.** Whatever app you read in (NeoReader, KOReader,
  Moon+ Reader) opens EPUB directly. There is no Kobo KEPUB or Kindle AZW3 step.
- **USB uses Android file transfer.** When you plug the Page into a computer it
  appears as an Android device (MTP), not a plain drive. Many people just drag
  files into the "Books" folder in the file-transfer window.
- **Wireless is often easier.** Calibre's built-in "content server" lets the Page
  browse and download your whole library over Wi-Fi from a browser or an app.
- **microSD works too.** Put a card in, copy books to it on a computer, and every
  reading app can see them.
