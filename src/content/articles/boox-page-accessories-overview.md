The Page needs a case, like any reader that goes in a bag. A microSD card is
genuinely useful here: it lets you keep a large sideloaded library separate from
app data, and it survives a factory reset. Bluetooth earbuds are worth it if you
use the speaker's text-to-speech or play audiobooks. Picks are grouped by type
below, with a note on which are cut for this device and which fit any reader.
