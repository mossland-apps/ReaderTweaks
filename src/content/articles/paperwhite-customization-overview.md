There is far less to change on a Kindle than on a Kobo. Amazon does not support
custom sleep images, screensaver folders or gesture remapping. What you can do:

- **Deal with the lock-screen ads.** Remove them with a one-time payment, or
  understand what a "with Special Offers" device does and does not show.
- **Add your own fonts.** Kindles read user fonts from a "fonts" folder on the
  device, the same idea as Kobo.
- **Adjust the reading layout.** Font size, boldness, line spacing, margins and
  alignment, all in the reading menu (the "Aa" button).
- **Turn off Whispersync, Popular Highlights and the like** in Settings, if you
  find them distracting.

For anything beyond that — full typography control, custom screens, per-gesture
actions — the honest answer is [KOReader](/devices/kindle/paperwhite/koreader/),
which means jailbreaking.
