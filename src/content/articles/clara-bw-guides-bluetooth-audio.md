steps:
  - instruction: Know what audio the Clara BW can and cannot play
    detail: >-
      The Clara BW plays Kobo audiobooks bought from the Kobo store, and
      audiobooks borrowed through the built-in OverDrive or Libby library link.
      It does not play your own MP3 or M4B audiobook files, and it has no
      speaker or headphone socket, so Bluetooth is the only output.
  - instruction: Put your headphones into pairing mode
    detail: >-
      Most earbuds enter pairing mode when you first open the case or hold a
      button until a light flashes; a speaker usually has a dedicated Bluetooth
      button. Check the accessory's own instructions. It must be discoverable,
      not just switched on.
  - instruction: Open Bluetooth on the Clara BW
    detail: >-
      On the home screen, open the menu, then Settings, then "Bluetooth". Turn
      Bluetooth on if it is not already. The Clara BW scans for nearby devices.
  - instruction: Select your headphones from the list
    detail: >-
      Tap your headphones or speaker when they appear. Pairing takes a few
      seconds. Once connected, the device is remembered and reconnects
      automatically next time both are on and in range.
    tip: >-
      If your earbuds do not appear, move them closer, make sure they are not
      already connected to a phone, and try the scan again. Only one source can
      hold a Bluetooth connection at a time.
  - instruction: Start an audiobook
    detail: >-
      Open an audiobook from "My Books". Playback controls, a sleep timer and a
      playback-speed setting are on the player screen. Volume is controlled from
      the player or the quick settings panel.
  - instruction: Disconnect or switch devices
    detail: >-
      To hand the headphones back to your phone, either turn Bluetooth off on
      the Clara BW or "Forget" the headphones under Settings, then Bluetooth.
      Leaving Bluetooth on uses noticeably more battery, so switch it off when
      you are only reading.
---

Audio on the Clara BW is Bluetooth-only and Kobo-only: store or library
audiobooks through headphones or a speaker you have paired. There is no way to
add your own audio files. If audiobooks are a large part of how you listen, that
limitation is worth knowing before you rely on this device for them.
