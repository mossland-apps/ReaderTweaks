The Go 6 is small and light and needs very little. A case that sleeps and wakes
the device on open and close is the one purchase most people should make, partly
because it saves battery. A microSD card keeps a large sideloaded library
separate from app data and safe through a factory reset. Bluetooth earbuds are
the way to hear audiobooks or text-to-speech, since the Go 6 has no speaker.
Picks are grouped by type below.
