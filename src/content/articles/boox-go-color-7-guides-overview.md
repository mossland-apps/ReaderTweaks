The Go Color 7 is an Android device, so most of the work is choosing and tuning
apps rather than fighting a locked ecosystem. This section covers setting up a
new one, installing the reading apps you want, and the display settings that make
Android apps usable on an e-ink screen.
[KOReader](/devices/boox/boox-go-color-7/koreader/) and
[Calibre](/devices/boox/boox-go-color-7/calibre/) have their own sections;
[Customization](/devices/boox/boox-go-color-7/customization/) covers fonts,
gestures and the per-app optimisation dialog.
