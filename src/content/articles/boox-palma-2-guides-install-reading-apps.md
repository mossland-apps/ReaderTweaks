steps:
  - instruction: Decide what you want it for
    detail: >-
      Because it is phone-shaped, people use the Palma 2 for more than books.
      Common picks are the Kindle and Kobo apps for store books, Libby for
      library loans, a general reader such as Moon+ Reader for your own files,
      and — the reason many buy it — a read-it-later app (Readwise Reader,
      Pocket, Instapaper) or an RSS reader for long-form articles without a bright
      phone screen. KOReader has its own section.
  - instruction: Install from the Play Store
    detail: >-
      Open the Play Store, search, and install as on a phone. If an app is
      missing, try the Play Store website in the browser, or sideload the APK from
      the developer's site.
  - instruction: Open the app once, then open BOOX's per-app settings
    detail: >-
      Launch the app so Android registers it. Then, from the Apps screen or the
      control centre, open its BOOX "optimisation" panel. This dialog controls
      how the app looks and behaves on e-ink.
  - instruction: Set the display and refresh options per app
    detail: >-
      For a text reading or article app choose a clean refresh mode (Regal or
      Normal), turn on "bleach" or "enhance contrast" only if text looks grey,
      and enable "full refresh on page turn" if you see heavy ghosting. For a
      scrolling app (RSS, a feed), a faster mode is more comfortable with full
      refresh every few screens.
  - instruction: Fix the DPI for the small screen
    detail: >-
      Many apps assume a larger phone and look cramped on the 6.13-inch screen.
      Nudge the app's DPI in the optimisation panel until buttons and text are a
      comfortable size, then reopen the app.
  - instruction: Control background behaviour
    detail: >-
      Under Settings, then "Power", restrict apps you open only occasionally so
      they do not sync in the background and drain the battery. Keep the one or
      two you use constantly exempt.
  - instruction: Turn off in-app animations
    detail: >-
      In each app's own settings, turn off page-turn animations, scrolling
      effects and auto-brightness, and set a plain light theme.
---

The Palma 2 runs the real Kindle, Kobo, Libby, read-it-later and RSS apps, which
is the point of a phone-shaped e-ink device — long-form reading without an LCD.
Each app needs two rounds of setup: its own account and settings, and BOOX's
per-app optimisation panel. On the small screen, the DPI setting is especially
worth adjusting so apps are not phone-cramped.
