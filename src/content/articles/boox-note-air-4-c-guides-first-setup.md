steps:
  - instruction: Charge the Note Air 4 C before setup
    detail: >-
      A new device often ships part-charged, and setup includes a system update.
      Plug it in with the supplied USB-C cable and give it time on the charger
      before you start.
  - instruction: Turn it on and connect to Wi-Fi
    detail: >-
      Press the power button on the top edge. Choose your language and time zone,
      then your Wi-Fi network and enter the password. The device needs Wi-Fi to
      update and to reach the Play Store.
  - instruction: Install any firmware update
    detail: >-
      Open Settings, then "Firmware Update", and apply anything offered. BOOX
      updates regularly, and the updates improve both how Android apps render on
      e-ink and how the pen behaves, so it is worth doing now.
  - instruction: Decide about a BOOX account
    detail: >-
      A BOOX (Onyx) account is used for the BOOX cloud — syncing notebooks, the
      reading position in the stock NeoReader app, and configuration backups
      across BOOX devices. On a note tablet the notebook sync is the main reason
      to sign in. It is still optional; you can also export notes as PDF instead.
  - instruction: Turn on the Google Play Store
    detail: >-
      Open the Apps screen and find "Play Store". On first launch, BOOX shows a
      screen to "enable Google Play"; tap through it, wait a minute or two while
      it registers the device with Google, then restart the device if prompted.
      Once done, sign in with your Google account.
    tip: >-
      If the Play Store says "device not certified" or apps will not install,
      restart the device, wait a few hours, and try again — Google's registration
      can take time to propagate. It almost always clears on its own.
  - instruction: Pair and set up the pen
    detail: >-
      The pen needs no charging or battery. Touch it to the screen and it works.
      In Settings, open the pen or handwriting section to set pressure, choose
      whether the side button erases, and calibrate if the ink lands slightly off
      the tip.
  - instruction: Learn the control centre
    detail: >-
      Swipe down from the top-right for the BOOX control centre — refresh-mode
      buttons, a colour/black-and-white toggle, front-light sliders (brightness
      and warm tone), and a full-screen refresh button that clears ghosting.
  - instruction: Set a comfortable default refresh mode
    detail: >-
      In the control centre, the global refresh mode is usually best on
      "Balanced" or "Regal" for reading. "HD" is sharpest but slowest; the faster
      modes are for scrolling and menus. Per-app settings override this, covered
      in a later guide.
---

Setting up the Note Air 4 C is a normal Android first-run with two extra steps:
enabling the Google Play Store, which BOOX ships switched off, and setting up the
pen. Once Google is signed in the device behaves like any Android tablet that
happens to have a colour e-ink screen. The BOOX account is optional but more
useful here than on a plain reader, because it syncs your notebooks.
