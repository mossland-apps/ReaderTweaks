steps:
  - instruction: Open the key-mapping settings
    detail: >-
      From the home screen, open Settings, then "Personalize", then "Key mapping"
      (on some firmware it is under "Reading settings"). You will see a list of
      the physical controls the InkPad Color 3 has.
  - instruction: Understand short press and long press
    detail: >-
      Each of the two bottom page-turn buttons has two actions you can set
      separately — a short press and a long press (holding the button for about a
      second). That gives you four assignable actions from two buttons.
  - instruction: Set the short presses for turning pages
    detail: >-
      Most people leave short press on the right button as "Next page" and the
      left as "Previous page". Swap them if you prefer, or if you usually hold
      the device in one particular hand.
  - instruction: Put the colour toggle and screen refresh on long press
    detail: >-
      This is the device where the long presses earn their keep. Assign one to
      the colour / black-and-white toggle so you can drop to sharp mono for a
      novel and back to colour for a comic without opening a menu, and the other
      to "Screen refresh" to clear ghosting on a busy colour page.
  - instruction: Save and test
    detail: >-
      The changes take effect immediately. Open a colour book and a plain one and
      confirm the toggle and refresh do what you expect. KOReader keeps its own
      separate mapping if you run it.
  - instruction: Note the other options
    detail: >-
      "Add bookmark", "Dictionary", "Rotate" and "Table of contents" are also
      available if you would rather use a long press for one of those.
---

Two bottom buttons with a short and long press each give you four actions. On the
InkPad Color 3 the standout long-press choices are the colour / black-and-white
toggle and a manual screen refresh — together they let you move between a sharp
mono novel and a colour comic without ever opening a menu.
