steps:
  - instruction: Create a notebook and pick a template
    detail: >-
      From the library, create a new notebook. Choose a template — plain, lined,
      dotted, grid, planners and many more. On a 7.3-inch page the wider-spaced
      lined and dotted templates are more comfortable than the dense ones. Each
      page can use its own template.
  - instruction: Learn the writing tools, colour and layers
    detail: >-
      The toolbar has several pens (ballpoint, fineliner, marker, pencil,
      calligraphy, brush), a highlighter, an eraser, and a selection tool for
      moving, resizing and cutting handwriting. The Move's Gallery 3 screen shows
      a wide colour range, so coloured pens and highlighters look close to what
      you would expect on paper. Layers let you keep, for example, a diagram
      separate from your annotations of it.
  - instruction: Write on an imported PDF
    detail: >-
      Open a PDF and write directly on it. Your marks are stored as an annotation
      layer over the original, so the source page is never altered. You can add
      pages to a PDF for extra working space. On the small screen, zoom in to
      write neatly, then zoom out to see the whole page.
  - instruction: Convert handwriting to text
    detail: >-
      In a notebook, open the page menu and choose "Convert to text", or select
      handwriting with the selection tool and convert just that. The result can
      be emailed or copied. Accuracy is good with clear printing and drops with
      fast cursive; it supports many languages, set per notebook.
  - instruction: Organise with folders and tags
    detail: >-
      The library uses folders, and you can add tags to notebooks and to
      individual pages, then filter by tag. A pocket device fills with quick
      notes fast, so tag as you go.
  - instruction: Export a notebook or annotated PDF
    detail: >-
      From a notebook or document menu, choose to email it, or export it as PDF
      or PNG. Colour is preserved in the export. With the desktop and mobile apps
      you can also open your notes there.
  - instruction: Know what it does not do
    detail: >-
      There is no app store and no third-party apps in the normal software. You
      cannot import notes from another brand of tablet directly. Full-text search
      of handwriting is limited. And everything lives in reMarkable's system —
      you export to PDF or text; there is no open notebook file without developer
      mode.
---

The Move's note-taking is the full reMarkable feature set — a wide range of
pens, layers, colour, per-page templates, and reliable handwriting-to-text if
you print rather than scrawl — on a screen small enough to hold in one hand. The
main adjustments for the size are using the wider-spaced templates and zooming
in to write on PDFs. Organisation is down to you, so use folders and tags early.
