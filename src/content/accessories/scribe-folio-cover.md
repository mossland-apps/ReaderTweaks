The pen holder is the reason to get a case for the Scribe — the pen's magnetic
attach to the bare device is weak and it falls off in a bag. Amazon's own
folios and the leather versions are expensive; third-party folios with a pen
loop cost much less and protect just as well. Check that the fold-back stand
holds a comfortable writing angle.
