Not optional in the long run. A tip lasts a few months of regular writing, less
with a textured screen protector. Felt tips give more paper-like drag and wear
faster; POM tips are slicker and last longer. BOOX's own tips and well-reviewed
third-party packs both work; avoid the cheapest unbranded ones, which can be
inconsistent.
