PocketBook's own "Shell" and "Origami" style covers fit the Verse (PB629)
exactly and include the sleep magnet. The Verse and Verse Pro share a body, so a
case sold for either usually fits both — check the listing names the model.
Third-party cases are cheaper; make sure the cut-outs clear the two page-turn
buttons on the front edge.
