Not optional in the long run. A tip lasts a few weeks to a few months depending
on how hard and how much you write, and less with a textured screen protector.
reMarkable's own tips and well-reviewed third-party packs both work; the packs
include a puller ring. The Paper Pro, Paper Pro Move and Paper Pure all use the
same current-generation tip — the reMarkable 2's older Marker takes its own.
