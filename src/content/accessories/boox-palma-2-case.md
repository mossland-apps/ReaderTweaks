Because the Palma 2 rides in a pocket or bag like a phone, a cover earns its
place — it stops the screen being scratched by keys and coins, and the sleep
magnet stops an Android device draining while it is shut. BOOX's own folio fits
the camera cut-out and volume rocker exactly; third-party Palma 2 cases are
cheaper and mostly fine. Some people prefer a slim back-shell with a grip loop
instead of a folio for true one-handed use.
