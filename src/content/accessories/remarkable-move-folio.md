On a device this small and portable a folio earns its place mainly for the
Marker loop — the pen's magnetic hold on the edge is light and it is easy to
lose in a bag. reMarkable's own folios are well made but expensive;
well-reviewed third-party folios cut for the Move protect just as well. Check
that it folds to a stable writing angle and does not add much bulk, since
compactness is the point of the Move.
