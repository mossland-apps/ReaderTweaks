A real trade-off on a writing device. A light matte protector adds scratch
resistance with little downside. A heavily textured "paper-feel" film makes
writing more pleasant and controlled, but softens text and wears your Marker tips
noticeably faster. Decide based on how much of your time is writing versus
reading.
