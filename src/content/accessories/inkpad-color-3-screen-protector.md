Optional, and the choice of film matters more on a colour screen. A thick matte
"paper-feel" protector adds haze that makes the already-muted Kaleido colour
look worse. If you want protection, pick a thin clear or only lightly matte
film. The screen is flush glass, tough and waterproof, so a protector is only
for scratch resistance — skip it if the device stays home.
