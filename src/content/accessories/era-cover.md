PocketBook's own "Shell" cover for the Era (PB700) fits exactly and includes the
sleep magnet. The Era and Era Color share a body, so a case sold for either
usually fits both — check the listing names the model. Third-party folios are
cheaper; make sure the design does not wrap over the raised right-hand grip
where the page-turn buttons are.
