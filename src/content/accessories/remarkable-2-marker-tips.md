Not optional in the long run. A tip lasts a few weeks to a few months depending
on how hard and how much you write, and less with a textured screen protector.
Make sure the pack is listed for the reMarkable 2 (or "reMarkable 1 and 2") —
the Paper Pro, Paper Pro Move and Paper Pure use a different tip. reMarkable's
own tips and well-reviewed third-party packs both work; packs include a puller
ring, and third-party ones are very cheap for this device.
