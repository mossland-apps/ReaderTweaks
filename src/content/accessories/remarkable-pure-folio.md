A folio is close to essential on a device this size. reMarkable's "Sleeve" and
"Book" folios for the Paper Pure are well made but cost a lot; well-reviewed
third-party folios sized for the Paper Pure protect just as well and usually
include a Marker loop. The Paper Pure's body is close in size to the old
reMarkable 2 but not identical, so check the listing names the Paper Pure
specifically rather than assuming an rM2 case fits.
