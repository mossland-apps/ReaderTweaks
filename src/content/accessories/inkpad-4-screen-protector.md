Optional, and harder to fit well at this size — a 7.8-inch screen shows every
bubble and speck. The InkPad 4's screen is flush glass, reasonably tough and
waterproof, so a protector is only for scratch resistance. If you carry it in a
loose bag with other things, it is worth the fiddly install; if it lives at home,
skip it.
