On a 7.8-inch reader a folio that folds back into a stand is genuinely useful —
it holds the InkPad Color 3 at an angle for long comic or cookbook sessions.
PocketBook's own "Shell" cover for the InkPad Color 3 (PB743) fits exactly and
has the sleep magnet. The InkPad Color 3 and InkPad 4 share a body, so a case
for either usually fits both; check the listing. Third-party folios are cheaper —
make sure the design clears the two page-turn buttons on the bottom bezel.
