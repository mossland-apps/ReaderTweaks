A genuine trade-off. If you write for long stretches, the added tooth makes it
more pleasant and controlled. In exchange, reading text looks a little less
crisp, and your pen tips wear out noticeably sooner. If you mostly read and
write only occasionally, skip it — the bare screen is already matte.
