A folio is close to essential on a device this size, and a used reMarkable 2
often arrives without one. reMarkable's own "Book" and "Folio" cases are well
made but pricey; because the reMarkable 2 has been out for years, there is a
huge range of well-reviewed third-party folios that protect just as well and
include a Marker loop. Check it folds to a stable writing angle and fits the
reMarkable 2 specifically — the newer Paper Pure is a slightly different size.
