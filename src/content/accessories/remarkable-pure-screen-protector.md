A real trade-off on a writing device, and the Paper Pure already ships with a
Gorilla Glass screen that has a paper-like texture, so a protector is less
necessary than on some tablets. A light matte film adds scratch resistance with
little cost. A heavily textured "paper-feel" film makes writing more pleasant
but reduces the contrast of an already-unlit screen and wears your Marker tips
noticeably faster. Decide based on how much of your time is writing versus
reading.
