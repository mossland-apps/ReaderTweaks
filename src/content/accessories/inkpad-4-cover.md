On a 7.8-inch reader a folio that folds back into a stand is genuinely useful —
it holds the InkPad 4 at an angle for long document sessions. PocketBook's own
"Shell" cover for the InkPad 4 (PB743) fits exactly and has the sleep magnet.
The InkPad 4 and InkPad Color 3 share a body, so a case for either usually fits
both; check the listing. Third-party folios are cheaper — make sure the design
clears the two page-turn buttons on the bottom bezel.
