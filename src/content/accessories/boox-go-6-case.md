On the Go 6 the sleep magnet is the thing that matters — an Android device left
awake in a bag drains, and auto-sleep on close prevents it. BOOX's own folio
fits exactly; third-party cases for the Go 6 are cheaper and mostly fine, since
there are no buttons to fit around.
