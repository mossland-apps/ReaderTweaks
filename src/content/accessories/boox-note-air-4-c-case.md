The pen holder is the main reason to get a case — the pen's magnetic attach to
the bare device is light and it falls off in a bag. BOOX's own folio fits
exactly and includes the sleep magnet, which matters on an Android device that
drains if left awake. Third-party folios with a pen loop cost less and protect
just as well; check the fold-back stand holds a comfortable writing angle.
