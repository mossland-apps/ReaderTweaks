More worthwhile on the Move than on a reMarkable that stays on a desk, because a
pocket device meets keys and coins. A thin clear or lightly matte film protects
without much downside. Be cautious with heavily textured "paper-feel" film here:
on the Move's colour screen it adds haze that makes the already-muted colour
look worse, and it wears Marker tips noticeably faster.
