fixes:
  - title: Check the warm light and its schedule
    rationale: >-
      The warm light adds an amber tone to the whole screen. If it is set high,
      or a schedule turns it up in the evening, white pages look yellow and
      colours shift warm.
    steps:
      - Swipe down from the top of the screen and slide the warm-light control all the way down.
      - >-
        Check whether a warm-light schedule is on. If it is, the tint will come
        back at the scheduled time; turn the schedule off or adjust it.
      - >-
        If an auto-adjusting light is on (on models that have it), turn it off
        while you test, so the screen does not change under you.
  - title: Turn the front light up, and set expectations for colour
    rationale: >-
      Colour e-ink uses a colour filter over the screen, which absorbs light.
      With the front light very low, colours look flat. Even at their best,
      colours are muted, like print on newspaper rather than a phone screen.
    steps:
      - Raise the brightness to around the middle of the slider and look at a colour book cover again.
      - Compare in good daylight, where colour e-ink looks its best.
      - >-
        If only your own books look grey, the file is the cause. A book or comic
        converted to greyscale stays grey — see the Calibre guide for keeping
        colour when converting comics.
    note: >-
      Photos in online listings are often edited or taken under bright light.
      Compare with a colour book cover from the Kindle store rather than with
      a product photo.
  - title: Contact Amazon about a yellow band that stays put
    rationale: >-
      Soon after the original Colorsoft launched in late 2024, some owners
      reported a yellowish band along the bottom of the screen that settings did
      not change. Amazon offered returns and replacements, and later production
      was reported to be fixed. A band like this is a hardware fault, not
      something you can fix yourself.
    steps:
      - >-
        Rule out the settings first: warm light off, schedule off, brightness at
        the middle. Look at a plain white page.
      - >-
        If the band is still there, take a clear photo of it on a white page.
      - >-
        Contact Amazon customer service through your Amazon account, under the
        order or under Devices. Describe the band and offer the photo. Within the
        return window or warranty, ask for a replacement.
    note: >-
      Do not press on the screen or try to "massage" the band away. It does not
      help and can damage the display.
recovery: >-
  Nothing here changes your books or settings beyond the light controls. If
  Amazon replaces the Kindle, your purchases, highlights and reading positions
  come back when you register the new one to the same account; books you loaded
  over USB need copying again.

Start with the warm light. It is behind more "yellow screen" reports than
anything else, and it takes seconds to rule out. If the colours are simply
softer than you hoped, that is how colour e-ink looks. The one case to take to
Amazon is a yellow band that stays in the same place whatever you change.
