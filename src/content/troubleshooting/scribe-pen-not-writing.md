symptoms:
  - The pen makes no mark on the screen at all.
  - Lines skip, break up, or only register when you press hard.
  - The ink appears a moment behind the pen tip.
  - The Premium Pen eraser or the barrel shortcut button does nothing.

  eraser and shortcut button not working is usually a settings or firmware

fixes:
  - title: Restart the Scribe and re-pair the pen
    rationale: >-
      A pen that suddenly makes no marks, or writes with lag, is almost always a
      software hang rather than a hardware fault. A restart clears it, and
      re-touching the pen re-establishes the pairing.
    steps:
      - >-
        Restart the Scribe from Settings, then Device Options, then Restart. If
        the screen is unresponsive, hold the power button for about 40 seconds
        until it restarts on its own.
      - >-
        After it boots, open the built-in tutorial notebook or a new notebook and
        touch the pen to the screen. It re-pairs automatically.
      - >-
        Try writing near the centre of the screen first. If the centre works but
        the edges do not, the problem is likely the tip (Fix 2), not the
        software.
      - >-
        If there is still no response anywhere, try the spare tip from the box in
        case the fitted one is damaged, then move to Fix 2.
  - title: Replace the pen tip
    rationale: >-
      Pen tips are a wear part. As one flattens it stops making reliable contact,
      which shows up as skipping, broken lines, or needing to bear down. A fresh
      tip usually fixes it completely.
    steps:
      - >-
        Use the small tip-removal tool from the box (or fine tweezers) to grip
        the tip and pull it straight out. It comes out with a firm, even pull.
      - Push a new tip straight in until it seats. It should sit flush and not wobble.
      - >-
        Test on a notebook page. Writing should feel light again and lines should
        be unbroken.
      - >-
        Order more tips when you are down to your last one. Amazon and third
        parties sell tip packs; the Premium and Basic pens use the same tips.
    note: >-
      A screen protector, especially a heavily textured "paper-feel" one, wears
      tips faster. If you use one, expect to replace tips more often.
  - title: Fix the eraser or shortcut button
    rationale: >-
      The eraser end and the barrel shortcut button are only on the Premium Pen,
      and both are controlled in settings. If they stopped working, it is usually
      a setting or a firmware bug rather than the pen.
    steps:
      - >-
        Confirm you have the Premium Pen — it has a flat eraser end and a button
        on the barrel. The Basic Pen has neither, so there is nothing to fix.
      - >-
        Open Settings, then "Pen", and check the shortcut button is assigned to
        an action such as "Eraser" or "Highlighter" and is not set to "Off".
      - >-
        Make sure the Scribe firmware is current, from Settings, then Device
        Options, then Software Updates. Eraser and button behaviour has been
        fixed in past updates.
      - >-
        Restart the Scribe. If the eraser still does nothing after a restart and
        a firmware check, contact Amazon — a pen with a dead eraser is usually
        replaced under warranty.
recovery: >-
  None of these steps touch your content. If a restart or tip change does not
  help and the pen still will not write anywhere, the pen or the digitiser may be
  faulty; the pen is covered by the Scribe's warranty and Amazon replaces it
  readily.

If the pen skips or needs pressure, replace the tip first — it is the answer
most of the time and takes a minute. If it makes no marks at all or lags,
restart the Scribe before assuming anything is broken.
