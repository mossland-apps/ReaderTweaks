An e-ink screen moves tiny pigment particles with an electric charge and then
holds them in place with no power. An LCD shines a backlight through a
fast-switching liquid-crystal layer. Almost every difference you notice follows
from that.

## Where e-ink wins

- **Reading in daylight.** It reflects ambient light like paper, so bright sun
  makes it more readable, not less.
- **Battery.** Holding a static page costs nothing; you charge in weeks, not
  hours.
- **Eye comfort for long sessions.** No backlight flicker, and a front light
  that bounces off the surface rather than shining at you.

## The trade-offs

- **Refresh speed.** Page turns take a fraction of a second and scrolling is
  rough. Fine for books, poor for the web.
- **Ghosting.** Faint remnants of the previous page until a full-screen refresh
  clears them.
- **Colour.** Colour e-ink exists but is muted and lower-resolution than the
  black-and-white layer on the same screen.

## Choosing

If your main use is reading text, e-ink is the better tool and the trade-offs
fade into the background. If you want a general tablet that also reads, an LCD
tablet will frustrate you less.
