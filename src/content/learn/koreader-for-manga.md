KOReader opens **CBZ** and **CBR** archives and image-only PDFs directly, and it
handles them better than most stock readers — but the defaults assume a novel.
A few changes make it a proper comic reader.

## Screen size matters first

Comics were drawn for a printed page. On a **6-inch** screen a full page is
small; you will be zooming into panels. **7 to 8 inches** is the comfortable
minimum for reading a page whole, and a **10-inch** device is close to print
size. No setting fixes a page that is physically too small — pick the device for
the job if you read a lot.

## The core settings

Open a comic, tap the bottom of the screen for the document menu:

- **Fit / zoom.** "Fit page" shows the whole page; "fit width" fills the screen
  width and you scroll or tap down the page. For a large screen, fit page. For
  6–7 inches, fit width plus panel-by-panel panning.
- **Page-turn direction.** For **manga**, set reading direction to
  **right-to-left** so a forward tap or swipe goes the way the art is drawn.
  Western comics stay left-to-right. This is a per-book setting KOReader usually
  remembers.
- **Double-page / two-page spread.** On a wide or landscape screen KOReader can
  show two pages side by side. Turn it off for manga (single pages are the norm)
  and on for Western comics in landscape if you want the spread.
- **Trim / crop.** If scans have a white border, auto-crop removes it and makes
  the art bigger.

## Refresh and ghosting

Comic pages are dense, so ghosting builds up faster than with text.

- Set **full-screen refresh** to every page, or every two pages if you can
  tolerate a little ghosting for faster turns.
- On BOOX and other Android devices, also set KOReader's own per-app refresh mode
  to a clean one (HD / Regal) and let KOReader manage full refresh rather than
  the system.

## Colour

On a colour e-ink screen (Kaleido, Gallery), KOReader shows colour pages in
colour — muted, at the panel's lower colour resolution, but real. Greyscale
manga looks identical in black-and-white mode, so only switch the screen to
colour for pages that have colour in them.

## The short version

Fit width with panel panning on a small screen, fit page on a big one; manga
right-to-left; double-page off for manga; auto-crop on; full refresh every page.
