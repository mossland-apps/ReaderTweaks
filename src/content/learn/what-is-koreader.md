KOReader is a free reading application maintained by volunteers. It runs on Kobo,
Kindle, PocketBook, BOOX and several other devices, sitting alongside — or
instead of — the software your reader shipped with.

## Why people install it

- **Typography control.** Fine-grained margins, line spacing, hyphenation and
  embedded-font handling that stock readers rarely expose.
- **One reading experience everywhere.** The same app, settings and shortcuts on
  every device you own.
- **Format range.** EPUB, PDF, CBZ, FB2, DjVu and more, with a genuinely usable
  PDF reflow mode.
- **Statistics and sync.** Reading-time tracking and progress sync across devices
  without an account tied to a store.

## Why you might not

- The interface is dense and takes an afternoon to learn.
- On locked-down devices (most Kindles) installing it is fiddly and can be
  undone by firmware updates.
- If the stock reader already does what you need, KOReader is optional, not
  mandatory.

## The short version

If you like adjusting how a page looks, or you own more than one reader, KOReader
is worth the setup time. If you just want to open a book and read, the stock
software is probably fine.
