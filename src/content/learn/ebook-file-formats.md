## The formats you will meet

- **EPUB** — the open standard. Reflowable text, works on everything except older
  Kindles directly. Keep your library here if you can.
- **KF8 / AZW3** — Amazon's format. Effectively EPUB in a different wrapper.
  Convert to this for Kindle.
- **MOBI** — the old Kindle format. Being retired; avoid for new files.
- **PDF** — fixed layout. Great for documents and comics, poor for novels on a
  small screen because the text cannot reflow.
- **CBZ / CBR** — zipped images for comics and manga.

## Rules of thumb

1. Store originals in **EPUB**. It converts cleanly to anything else later.
2. Convert **to AZW3** only when sending to a Kindle, and keep the EPUB.
3. Do not convert a **PDF** to EPUB expecting good results — the layout rarely
   survives. Use your reader's PDF reflow mode instead.
4. Every conversion can introduce small formatting errors. Convert once, from the
   best source you have, not repeatedly.

## DRM

Books bought from a store are often locked to that store's app. That is a
licensing question, not a format one, and this site does not cover removing it.
