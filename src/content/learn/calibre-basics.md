Calibre is a free program for Windows, macOS and Linux that acts as the home for
your ebook collection. Think of it as the library catalogue; your reader is just
one place books get sent to.

## The mental model

- Your books live in a **Calibre library folder** on your computer.
- Calibre stores **metadata** — titles, authors, covers, series — separately from
  the files, and can fix it in bulk.
- When you connect a reader, Calibre shows it as another shelf you can copy books
  to and from.

## The three tasks that cover most use

1. **Add books.** Drag files in. Calibre reads the metadata and downloads better
   covers and descriptions on request.
2. **Convert formats.** Right-click a book, choose Convert, pick EPUB for most
   readers or AZW3 for Kindle. Calibre keeps every format it has made.
3. **Send to device.** Connect over USB, select books, click Send to Device.
   Add the plugins for wireless sending later if you want it.

## Worth knowing

- Keep the library folder in your normal backups. Losing it loses your
  organisation, not just the files.
- The built-in metadata editor is faster than any web catalogue once you learn
  the bulk-edit dialog.
