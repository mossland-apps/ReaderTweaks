The Colorsoft is a Paperwhite-sized Kindle with a colour screen. Text is as
sharp as on the black-and-white Paperwhite (300 ppi), while colour is shown at
half that sharpness (150 ppi) and in soft, muted tones rather than the bright
colours of a phone. That makes it good for book covers, colour highlights,
comics, children's books and charts in PDFs. It is not a replacement for a
tablet.

Like every Kindle it is a closed device, so most of the useful work happens
around it: getting your own books and comics on with Send to Kindle or Calibre,
understanding what colour can and cannot do, and fixing the screen and Wi-Fi
problems that come up.

**Which Colorsoft is this?** The specifications above are for the 2026 model,
which shares the 2026 Paperwhite's thinner 6.8 mm body. Amazon's listing says it
is waterproof (IPX8), comes without lock-screen ads, has a battery you can have
replaced, and charges in under 2.5 hours with a 20 W USB-C adapter. A
**Signature Edition** adds 32 GB and works with Amazon's Page-Turn Cover and
charging dock. Earlier Colorsoft models from 2024 and 2025 are still widely sold,
and almost everything on these pages applies to them too. A Kids version is also
sold.

**KOReader:** possible on an earlier Colorsoft on firmware 5.18.1 up to 5.19.6,
using the Véra jailbreak, although colour support in KOReader on this screen is
still rough. We have not found a public jailbreak for the newer 5.20 firmware or
for the 2026 model at the time of writing. The KOReader section explains the
details.
