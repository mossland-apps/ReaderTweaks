The Page is the BOOX for people who mainly want to read. It has a 7-inch
black-and-white screen, a wide grip with two physical page-turn buttons, a
front light with a warm setting, a fingerprint reader in the power button, and a
speaker with Bluetooth for audiobooks and text-to-speech.

Underneath it is still full Android 11 with the Google Play Store, so it runs
the Kindle app, the Kobo app, Libby, KOReader and anything else — with the
usual BOOX setup for making those apps look right on e-ink. These pages cover
that, plus KOReader, Calibre, customization and the problems that come up.
