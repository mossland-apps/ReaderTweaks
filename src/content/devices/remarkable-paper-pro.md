The Paper Pro is a writing device first. It has an 11.8-inch screen, colour, a
front light, and the Marker pen, and the point of it is handwritten notes,
templates and PDF markup — not reading an ebook library. Its EPUB support is
basic and there is no store, so as a reader it is weak next to a Kobo or Kindle.

It runs reMarkable's own Linux system, "Codex", with developer-mode SSH access
that the community uses for custom templates, third-party sync and even KOReader.
Some cloud features need a paid reMarkable Connect subscription. These pages
cover setup, getting documents on, the note-taking, customization, and the
problems that come up.
