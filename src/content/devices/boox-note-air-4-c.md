The Note Air 4 C is BOOX's mid-size colour note tablet: a 10.3-inch Kaleido 3
colour e-ink screen, a magnetic stylus, a warm front light, a speaker, and full
Android 13 with the Google Play Store. It is aimed at the same job as the Kindle
Scribe and the reMarkable Paper Pro — handwritten notes and PDF markup on a big
screen — but as an open Android device that also runs the Kindle app, the Kobo
app, Libby and KOReader.

That flexibility comes with more to set up: refresh modes, per-app optimisation,
and choosing between BOOX's own note app and a third-party one. These pages cover
setup, the reading apps, the stylus and note-taking, KOReader, Calibre,
customization and troubleshooting.
