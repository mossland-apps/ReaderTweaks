The Paperwhite is the reader most people mean when they say "Kindle". It is a
closed device — you cannot change much on the Kindle itself — so most of the
useful work happens around it: getting your own EPUB books on with Send to
Kindle or Calibre, managing your library on a computer, deciding what to do
about the lock-screen ads, and fixing the Wi-Fi and sync problems that come up
most.

**Which Paperwhite is this?** Amazon redesigned the Paperwhite in October 2026:
thinner and lighter, with a flush-front screen, the highest contrast of any
Paperwhite, and up to 12 weeks of battery. The specifications above are for that
2026 model. A **Signature Edition** adds 32 GB, an aluminium back, an
auto-adjusting light, double-tap page turns and dock charging, and it is the
only Paperwhite that works with Amazon's new Page-Turn Cover. The previous 2024
model (12th generation, 211 g) is still widely sold at a discount, and almost
everything on these pages applies to both.

**KOReader:** possible on a 2024 Paperwhite (or Paperwhite 5) on firmware 5.19.6
or lower, using the Véra jailbreak. We have not found a public jailbreak for the
newer 5.20 firmware or for the 2026 model at the time of writing. The KOReader
section explains the details.
