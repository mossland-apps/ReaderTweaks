The Paperwhite is the reader most people mean when they say "Kindle". It is a
closed device — you cannot change much on the Kindle itself — so most of the
useful work happens around it: getting your own EPUB books on with Send to
Kindle or Calibre, managing your library on a computer, deciding what to do
about the lock-screen ads, and fixing the Wi-Fi and sync problems that come up
most. Running KOReader is possible but involved, and covered honestly in its own
section.
