The Libra Colour sits in a sweet spot for people who like to change things: it
has the physical buttons and grip that make one-handed reading comfortable, a
colour screen that is genuinely useful for comics and highlights, and firmware
that tolerates sideloading, alternative fonts and KOReader without much fuss.

These pages cover getting it set up the way you want — from a clean first-boot
configuration through KOReader, Calibre, fonts, gestures and the problems that
come up most often.
