The Clara BW is small, light and inexpensive, with the same current-generation
6-inch screen as the rest of the 2024 Kobo line. It has no page-turn buttons and
no waterproofing, and its screen is black-and-white only. What it does well is be
a plain, fast reader that pockets easily and takes KOReader and sideloaded books
without a fuss. It also has Bluetooth for audiobooks.

These pages cover setting it up the way you want — first-run configuration,
KOReader, Calibre, fonts and sleep screens, and the problems that come up most.
