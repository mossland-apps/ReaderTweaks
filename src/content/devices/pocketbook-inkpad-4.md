The InkPad 4 is PocketBook's large 7.8-inch reader. The extra screen area is the
point: full-size PDF pages, comic panels, sheet music and textbooks are all
readable without the constant zooming a 6-inch screen forces. It keeps the
PocketBook essentials — physical page-turn buttons on the bottom bezel, a
warm-adjustable front light, IPX8 waterproofing, a built-in speaker with
Bluetooth and text-to-speech, and near-universal format support.

At 265 grams it is heavier than a pocket reader but light for its size, and the
300-ppi screen keeps small text sharp. KOReader installs by copying one file to
a folder, no jailbreak, and its PDF tools are a strong reason to add it here.
These pages cover setup, loading books, PDFs and documents, audio, customization
and troubleshooting.
