The Era is PocketBook's 7-inch reader: a 300-ppi Carta screen, side-mounted
page-turn buttons like a Kobo Libra, and an IPX8 waterproof body. Its
distinguishing feature is sound — unlike most e-readers and unlike the Verse
Pro, it has an actual built-in speaker as well as Bluetooth, so it can read
books aloud with text-to-speech and play your own MP3 and M4B audiobooks
without headphones.

The stock software is more capable than Kobo's or Amazon's: it opens almost
every format directly, handles Adobe-DRM library books on the device, and lets
you remap the buttons. KOReader installs by copying one file to a folder — no
jailbreak. These pages cover setup, loading books, audio, customization and
troubleshooting.
