The Palma 2 is shaped like a phone and runs full Android 13, but it has no SIM
slot and is not a phone — it is a pocket-sized e-ink reader you can hold in one
hand and slip into a jacket. It has a black-and-white 6.13-inch screen, a
front light with a warm setting, a microSD slot, a speaker, a fingerprint
reader, and a rear camera meant for scanning documents and pages into notes.

Because it is Android with the Play Store, it runs the Kindle app, the Kobo app,
Libby, KOReader, and — since it is phone-shaped — plenty of people also use it
for read-it-later apps, RSS and messaging without a bright LCD. The trade-offs
are the usual BOOX ones plus a small screen. These pages cover setup, the
reading apps, KOReader, Calibre, customization and troubleshooting.
