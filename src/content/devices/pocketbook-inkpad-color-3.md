The InkPad Color 3 is the colour version of PocketBook's large reader. It uses an
E Ink Kaleido 3 screen: the sharp 300-ppi black-and-white layer of a normal
InkPad, with a colour layer over it that shows about 4096 colours at a softer
150 ppi. On a 7.8-inch page that is enough for comics, magazines, children's
books, maps and highlighted study notes.

Everything else is standard PocketBook — bottom page-turn buttons, a
warm-adjustable front light, IPX8 waterproofing, a built-in speaker with
Bluetooth and text-to-speech, and format support that opens almost anything
without conversion. KOReader installs by copying one file to a folder, no
jailbreak. These pages cover setup, loading books, getting the most from the
colour screen, audio, customization and troubleshooting.
