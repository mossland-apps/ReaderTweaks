The Verse Pro's strengths are format range and audio. It opens EPUB, PDF, DjVu,
FB2, DOCX, CBZ, MOBI and more without any conversion, reads books aloud with
text-to-speech, and plays your own MP3 and M4B audio files — something no Kobo
or Kindle does. It also has physical page-turn buttons you can remap,
waterproofing, and built-in Adobe DRM support for library books.

The stock software is more capable than Kobo's or Amazon's, and KOReader
installs by copying one file to a folder — no jailbreak. These pages cover
setup, getting books on, audio, customization and the problems that come up.
