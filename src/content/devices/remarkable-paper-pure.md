The Paper Pure is reMarkable's plain 10.3-inch writing tablet, and the direct
replacement for the discontinued reMarkable 2. It keeps the formula that made
that device popular — a matte monochrome E Ink screen with very low writing
latency, no front light, no distractions — on newer internals: a faster
processor, more memory and storage, a larger replaceable battery, and a
screws-and-snaps design meant to be repaired rather than glued shut.

It uses the current-generation Marker and Marker Plus (the ones that charge
wirelessly on the device edge), not the reMarkable 2's pens. As an ebook reader
it is basic — EPUB support only, no store — so it is not a Kobo or Kindle
substitute. It runs reMarkable's Linux system with a developer mode the
community uses for custom templates and KOReader. Some cloud features need a
paid reMarkable Connect subscription. These pages cover setup, getting documents
on, the note-taking, customization and troubleshooting.
