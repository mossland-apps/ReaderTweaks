The Paper Pro Move is the small reMarkable — a 7.3-inch colour E Ink screen, a
front light, and the Marker, in a body light enough to hold in one hand or slip
into a jacket pocket. Like the rest of the line it is a writing device first:
handwritten notes, templates and PDF markup, synced through a reMarkable
account. Its colour screen uses E Ink Gallery 3, which shows a wider, richer
range of colour than the Kaleido panels on most colour e-readers, but refreshes
more slowly.

As an ebook reader it is weak — basic EPUB support, no store, a small page — so
it does not replace a Kobo or Kindle. It runs reMarkable's Linux system with a
developer mode that the community uses for custom templates and, with effort,
KOReader. Some cloud features need a paid reMarkable Connect subscription. These
pages cover setup, getting documents on, the note-taking, customization and
troubleshooting.
