The Verse is the plain version of PocketBook's 6-inch reader. It keeps the parts
that make a PocketBook a PocketBook — physical page-turn buttons you can remap, a
front light with a separate warm-tone slider, a microSD slot, built-in Adobe DRM
for library books, and a format list that opens EPUB, PDF, FB2, DjVu, DOCX, CBZ
and MOBI without conversion — and drops the extras. There is no speaker,
Bluetooth, text-to-speech or waterproofing; the screen is 212 ppi rather than
the Verse Pro's 300.

If you only read text and copy your own files on, the Verse does that job for
less money. KOReader installs by copying one file to a folder, no jailbreak.
These pages cover setup, loading books, library loans, customization and the
problems that come up.
