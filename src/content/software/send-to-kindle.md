Send to Kindle takes an EPUB, PDF, DOCX or TXT file and delivers it to your
Kindle library over Wi-Fi. EPUB files are converted to Amazon's own format
during delivery. It is the simplest way to sideload on a modern Kindle and does
not need a computer if you use the phone app.
